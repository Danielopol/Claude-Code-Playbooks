# AI-Shaped Readiness Advisor

Assess whether your product work is **"AI-first"** (using AI to automate existing tasks faster) or **"AI-shaped"** (fundamentally redesigning how product teams operate around AI capabilities). Evaluate readiness across **5 essential PM competencies for 2026**, identify gaps, and recommend which capability to build first.

**Key Distinction:** AI-first is cute (using Copilot to write PRDs faster). AI-shaped is survival (building a durable "reality layer" that both humans and AI trust, orchestrating AI workflows, compressing learning cycles).

## AI-First vs. AI-Shaped

| Dimension | AI-First (Cute) | AI-Shaped (Survival) |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Mindset** | Automate existing tasks | Redesign how work gets done |
| **Goal** | Speed up artifact creation | Compress learning cycles |
| **AI Role** | Task assistant | Strategic co-intelligence |
| **Advantage** | Temporary efficiency | Defensible competitive moat |
| **Example** | "Copilot writes PRDs 2x faster" | "AI validates hypotheses in 48hrs vs. 3wks" |

If a competitor can replicate your AI usage by throwing bodies at it, it's not differentiation — it's efficiency (which becomes table stakes within months).

## The 5 Essential PM Competencies (2026)

### 1. Context Design
Building a durable "reality layer" both humans and AI trust.
- Constraints registry, operational glossary, evidence standards
- Two-layer memory architecture (short-term conversational + long-term persistent)
- Context boundaries (what to persist vs. retrieve)
- 5 Diagnostic Questions: What decision does this support? Can retrieval replace persistence? Who owns the boundary? What fails if excluded? Are we fixing structure or avoiding it?

### 2. Agent Orchestration
Repeatable, traceable AI workflows (not one-off prompts). Research → synthesis → critique → decision → log rationale. Each step shows its work; workflows run consistently; prompts are version-controlled.

### 3. Outcome Acceleration
Using AI to compress **learning cycles** (not just speed up tasks). Eliminate validation lag (PoL probes in days, not weeks), remove approval delays, cut meeting overhead.

### 4. Team-AI Facilitation
Redesigning team systems so AI operates as co-intelligence. Review norms, evidence standards (AI cites sources), decision authority (AI recommends, humans decide), psychological safety.

### 5. Strategic Differentiation
New customer capabilities, workflow rewiring, economics competitors can't match (10x cost advantage through AI).

## Maturity Levels (Level 1-4 per Competency)

- **Level 1 (AI-First):** Ad-hoc, individual, efficiency only
- **Level 2 (Emerging):** Early capabilities, informal practices
- **Level 3 (Transitioning):** Redesign underway, structured practices emerging
- **Level 4 (AI-Shaped):** Strategic transformation, durable competitive advantage

## Application

Ask 8 context questions, then 5 one-per-competency scoring questions. Score Level 1-4 on each. Synthesize into a maturity profile.

**Priority Logic:**
1. Context Design is foundational — if Level 1-2, prioritize first
2. Agent Orchestration enables Outcome Acceleration — prioritize next if context is solid
3. Team-AI Facilitation is parallel — required for scale
4. Strategic Differentiation requires Levels 3+ on others — don't focus here until foundation is built

Deliver an action plan (week-by-week) for the priority competency with success criteria.

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Mistaking Efficiency for Differentiation** — If a competitor could match by adding headcount, it's not a moat
2. **Skipping Context Design** — Workflows without durable context are fragile
3. **Individual Usage, Not Team Transformation** — Can't scale if only you're AI-shaped
4. **Focusing on Tools, Not Workflows** — Tool choice doesn't matter; organizational redesign does
5. **Speed Over Learning** — AI helps you ship the wrong thing faster unless you compress learning cycles
