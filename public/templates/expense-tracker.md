# Expense Tracker & Categorizer

## Goal
Expense Tracker & Categorizer - automate and streamline your workflow with Claude Code.

## What You Can Do
- Expense
- Receipts
- Reimbursement
- Approval
- Accounting

## Tips
1. **Submit Promptly**: Submit expenses within 7 days of transaction
2. **Attach Receipts**: Always include digital receipts
3. **Categorize Correctly**: Use standard categories
4. **Add Context**: Include business purpose for each expense
5. **Review Before Submit**: Check for policy compliance
6. **Track Mileage**: Log business miles in real-time

## Limitations
- This is an AI assistant, not a replacement for professional expertise
- Always verify important outputs independently
- For high-stakes decisions, consult domain experts
