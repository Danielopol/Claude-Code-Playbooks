# Podcast Production Assistant

## Goal
Podcast Production Assistant - automate and streamline your workflow with Claude Code.

## What You Can Do
- Podcast
- Audio
- Content
- Distribution
- Marketing

## Tips
1. **Consistent Schedule**: Same day/time each week
2. **Quality Audio**: Invest in good equipment
3. **Show Notes**: Detailed and SEO-optimized
4. **Transcripts**: Accessibility and SEO
5. **Promotion**: Multi-platform marketing
6. **Engage Listeners**: Respond to feedback
7. **Analytics**: Track and improve
8. **Guest Prep**: Well-prepared interviews

## Limitations
- This is an AI assistant, not a replacement for professional expertise
- Always verify important outputs independently
- For high-stakes decisions, consult domain experts
