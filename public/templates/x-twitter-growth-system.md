# X (Twitter) Growth System

A milestone-based system to grow from 0 to 100K followers through daily consistency.

## The System in 8 Lines

1. **Connect** - Follow people in your niche. Follow-for-follow works when starting from zero.
2. **Reply guy forever** - Keep replying on big and small creators' posts. Every reply must be valuable.
3. **Post 2-5x daily** - Tweets, threads, articles. More content = more surface area.
4. **DM people** - Real conversations. Real value. Real relationships.
5. **Cross-platform** - Reddit, LinkedIn, Slack, Discord. Put your stuff where people already are.
6. **Engagement group** - Create a win-win group where everyone boosts each other.
7. **Daily consistency** - The algorithm rewards showing up. Miss a day and it notices.
8. **Stay unique** - Don't AI slop your content. People follow people, not templates.

---

## Daily Foundation (Every Milestone)

Do this every single day:

- [ ] Leave 10 genuine comments on posts in your niche
- [ ] Send 3-5 DMs to target audience (value first, never pitch)
- [ ] Reply to every comment on your posts (faster = better)

---

## Milestone 1: 0 → 200 Followers

**Goal: Get people to know you exist.**

### Daily Actions
- [ ] Follow 50 people per day in your ICP (expect 30-40% follow back)
- [ ] Join and participate in 3-5 X Communities (AI, automation, productivity, your niche)
- [ ] Post 1x per day about your project
- [ ] DM people with something real, not a pitch

### DM Template
```
Saw your post about [topic]. I built a free [resource] that does [specific thing] — no signup, just copy-paste. Thought you'd find it useful.
```

### What NOT to Do
Don't focus on posting content yet. Nobody's watching. Focus on comments and DMs.

---

## Milestone 2: 200 → 500 Followers

**Goal: Reach bigger audiences.**

Everything from Milestone 1 stays. Add these:

### Daily Actions
- [ ] Follow 20-30 people per day (reduced from 50)
- [ ] Reply-guy on 5-10 big creators (10K+ followers in your niche)
- [ ] Reply to their posts within the first hour with something useful
- [ ] Post 2x per day - one specific use case per post
- [ ] Start or join an engagement group (10 creators at your level)

### Engagement Group Rules
Everyone replies, likes, and bookmarks each other's posts within 10 minutes. This signals to the algorithm your content is worth showing.

### Post Format
```
[Audience]: this [playbook/tool/resource] [specific benefit] in [timeframe]. Copy-paste, no code: [link]
```

---

## Milestone 3: 500 → 1,000 Followers

**Goal: Feed your real audience.**

Everything from Milestone 2 stays. Add these:

### Daily Actions
- [ ] Post 2x daily at peak hours (7-9 AM and 5-7 PM ET)
- [ ] One playbook use case + one question or tip
- [ ] Ask engagement questions ("Founders, what are you building today?")

### Weekly Actions
- [ ] Record 1 Loom walkthrough of a top playbook (show before/after)
- [ ] Post in 1 Reddit community (r/ClaudeAI, r/ChatGPT, r/smallbusiness, r/marketing, etc.)

### What to Change
Drop the engagement group if posts get natural traction. Keep reply-guying — that never stops working.

---

## Milestone 4: 1,000 → 100,000 Followers

**Goal: Repetition. No new strategy.**

You already have everything you need. The system doesn't change — you just keep running it.

### Daily Actions
- [ ] Post at least 2x daily. Every day. No breaks.
- [ ] Reply to every comment. Fast (within 3 hours max)
- [ ] 10+ comments on other people's posts
- [ ] 3-5 value DMs
- [ ] Reply guy on big creators

### Weekly Actions
- [ ] 1 Loom walkthrough
- [ ] 1 Reddit post
- [ ] Screenshot and share 1 user win
- [ ] 1 playbook breakdown post

### Content Math
One playbook = one post. If you have 100+ playbooks, that's 3+ months of content. Use it. Then cycle back and do it again.
