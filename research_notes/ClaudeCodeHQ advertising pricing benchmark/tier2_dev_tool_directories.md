> **Method and verification caveat (read first).** In this session the egress proxy blocked direct fetches of almost every target site: openalternative.co, devhunt.org, uneed.best, saashub.com, libhunt.com, dirstarter.com, plus web.archive.org and curl to about 25 other directories. The figures below therefore come from WebSearch result pages. The search tool returns a summary of the indexed page content, not the raw page. A price attributed to an official URL means the search tool reported it from that URL's indexed content. No researcher loaded the page directly. Confidence levels follow from that:
> - **Medium** = official page (or founder post) as surfaced by search, ideally corroborated by a second source.
> - **Low** = third-party aggregator only, or conflicting sources.
> - **High** is not used anywhere, because no page was loaded directly.

## Rate list: what products do these sites sell, at what price, billing, placement, exclusivity and traffic?

### Takeaway
Recurring site-wide sponsorships at niche dev and indie directories cluster at about **$147–$597/month** (OpenAlternative $147/$297/$597; Uneed $297/month sidebar; Startup Fame $149/month; SaaSHub $99/month featured). Featured category or listing placements run about **$99–$197/month**. Paid launch or skip-queue listings are **one-time $15–$149**. Outliers sit well above that band: BetaList's single exclusive sponsor is $4,999/month, and the most direct Claude-ecosystem comparable, claudemarketplaces.com, charges $1,499/month across at most 8 sponsors. ClaudeCodeHQ's $350–$900/month sponsorships sit in the upper half of the indie-directory band and below the Claude-specific comparable. Its $199/yr listing is cheap next to $197/month featured listings, though it is pricier than one-time launch fees.

### Inferences
- **Price band for ClaudeCodeHQ's closest comparables.** At 26.8k visitors and 49.1k pageviews, ClaudeCodeHQ is roughly 1/8–1/18 the stated pageviews of OpenAlternative (900K). OpenAlternative's top tier is $597/month, so by straight traffic proportionality ClaudeCodeHQ's $350–$900/month asks are high. Audience specificity offsets some of that: claudemarketplaces.com charges $1,499/month per slot for a Claude-specific audience, with 8 slots and ~1,700 clicks/month. This is an estimate, not a sourced fact.
- **Implied cost per click at Claude Marketplaces.** $1,499 / 1,700 clicks is ≈ $0.88 per click. This is derived arithmetic from the advertised figures.
- **Implied CPM at OpenAlternative Silver.** $147 / ~90k impressions is ≈ $1.63 CPM. That is very low, and it suggests broad OSS-directory display inventory is priced cheaply. The Claude-specific vertical commands a premium. This is derived arithmetic.
- **Common structure.** Most sites run 3 tiers: a featured listing about $100–$200/month, a sitewide sponsor about $300–$600/month, and a newsletter slot about $250/issue. Slot caps or scarcity messaging are common: Uneed "1 spot left", Startup Fame "limited spots", BetaList's one exclusive sponsor, Claude Marketplaces' max 8.
- **One-time vs recurring.** Launch directories (DevHunt, Uneed, Microlaunch, Fazier, TinyLaunch, BetaList submit) sell one-time $15–$149 launches. Recurring monthly products are the sponsorship and featured tiers. ClaudeCodeHQ's $199/yr listing is a hybrid, priced above one-time launch fees but far below $99–$197/month featured listings.
- **Self-serve vs. apply.** Self-serve checkout appears standard: OpenAlternative's advertise flow, Uneed's per-issue purchase, Claude Marketplaces' "same-day go-live", SaaSHub's "no long-term contract". Newsletter-first properties (Console.dev, selfh.st main slot) quote on request. This is inferred from page descriptions; checkout flows were not observed directly.

## Publishing practices: stats, self-serve vs. apply, discounts, waitlists

### Takeaway
The more transparent directories publish traffic claims on the advertise page itself: OpenAlternative (900K pageviews / 220K visitors and per-placement view shares), Uneed ("125K+ founders and makers", "26,000+" newsletter), and Claude Marketplaces (~1,700 clicks/month, 13% share of voice). They pair those claims with scarcity cues and self-serve checkout. Newsletter-led properties (Console.dev) keep rate cards private.

### Cited Findings
- OpenAlternative's advertise page shows pageviews, visitors, and the per-placement percentage of pageviews where each ad appears. — [OpenAlternative advertise](https://openalternative.co/advertise)
- Uneed's advertise page headline is "Reach 125K+ Founders & Makers Every Month". Its pricing snapshots show "1 spot left" on the sidebar and multi-issue newsletter bundle discounts ($249 / $448 / $597). — [Uneed advertise](https://www.uneed.best/advertise)
- Claude Marketplaces publishes average clicks (~1,700/month), share of voice (~13%), a sponsor cap (max 8), monthly billing, cancel anytime, and same-day go-live. — [Claude Marketplaces advertise](https://claudemarketplaces.com/advertise)
- Founding or launch discounts appear as coupon codes: Microlaunch LAUNCH20 ($49→$39) and LAUNCH-PLUS-20 ($99→$79). — [Microlaunch premium](https://microlaunch.net/premium). Others are struck-through sale prices: Fazier ($39→$29, $69→$49) — [Fazier submit](https://fazier.com/submit); Uneed Pro early-bird $99 vs $139 list — [Uneed help](https://help.uneed.best/getting-started/uneed-pro).
- Queues and waitlists are used to drive paid upgrades. DevHunt's free launch wait is cited at ~6 months — [launchdirectories DevHunt](https://launchdirectories.com/directory/devhunt). Uneed reportedly closed its free queue on 2026-08-17 — [noonlaunch Uneed review](https://noonlaunch.com/blog/uneed-review) (unverified). BetaList is now paid-only — [wheretosubmit](https://wheretosubmit.org/guides/betalist-submission-guide).
- Console.dev's rates are "media kit on request", and most dev newsletters now share rate cards by email. — [daily.dev business guide](https://business.daily.dev/resources/best-developer-newsletters-to-sponsor/)

### Inferences
- If ClaudeCodeHQ publishes its pricing, the convention among comparables is to show:
  - visitors and pageviews on the advertise page;
  - per-placement visibility (share of pageviews) or average clicks;
  - an explicit slot cap ("max N sponsors", "X spots left");
  - monthly billing with cancel-anytime terms;
  - multi-issue or multi-month bundle discounts.

### Gaps
- No live checkout pages were observed, so minimum commitments and refund terms are unconfirmed.
- Whether OpenAlternative's stats are live analytics embeds or static text could not be confirmed.
