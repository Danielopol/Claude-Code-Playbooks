## Q1. Per-product rate cards: price, billing, audience, placement, implied CPM

### Takeaway
Few large AI and dev newsletters publish prices any more. TLDR, The Rundown, ByteByteGo, AlphaSignal, The Neuron, Refactoring, Pointer, Console.dev and Latent Space are all "contact for pricing" or media-kit-on-request. Where prices are published or reliably reported, newsletter placements cost about **$13–$50 per 1,000 subscribers per send**, or about **$35–$50 per 1,000 opens**:
- Ben's Bites: $2,500 main ad for 140k subscribers.
- Cooperpress JavaScript Weekly (2022): $3,180 for 179k subscribers.
- Changelog podcast: $2,500/week for 2 episodes, with a 4-week minimum.

Display networks for developer audiences are much cheaper: EthicalAds charges $3.10–$7.50 CPM (Q4 2026), and Carbon $6 CPM.

### Inferences
- **Site-wide card vs display networks.** If the card renders on all 49,100 page views, $900/month is an effective **~$18 CPM**. That is about 2.4x EthicalAds' top AI/ML US-tier rate ($7.50), 3x Carbon ($6) and 3.9x EthicalAds' AI/ML Run of Network ($4.60).
  - At those network rates, $900 buys 120,000 impressions (EthicalAds US-tier), 150,000 (Carbon) or ~195,650 (EthicalAds RON).
  - Carbon's ~1M impressions/month minimum (~$6,000) and Stack Overflow's $10k–$15k/month minimum mean those channels are not reachable at ClaudeCodeHQ's price point. EthicalAds' $1,000 minimum is the closest substitute in budget.
- **Site-wide card vs newsletters.** The ~$18 CPM sits at the low end of newsletter per-subscriber CPMs ($13–$50), close to Ben's Bites ($17.86) and JavaScript Weekly ($17.78).
  - But a page view is not an opened email. Per-open newsletter CPMs ($35–$50) are 2–3x higher, and email placements carry dedicated copy and attention.
  - A defensible pricing story would rest on audience specificity (Claude Code users actively choosing workflows) rather than raw CPM.
- **Category cards.** Their effective CPM depends entirely on category-page traffic, which was not supplied.
  - At $350–$500/month, a category needs ≈47k–67k monthly impressions to match EthicalAds' $7.50 US-tier CPM, or ≈19k–27k to match the ~$18 newsletter per-subscriber CPM.
  - A single category is unlikely to receive that from a 49,100-PV site. So category cards likely price well above developer-network CPMs. They need to be justified on intent and exclusivity, or repriced lower or bundled.
- **Price points small advertisers would compare against.** The cheapest entry tickets in the dev-media market sit in the same band as ClaudeCodeHQ's $350–$900: small dev newsletters (€160–€350/issue; DevOps'ish $750/week), EthicalAds' $1,000 minimum, and Laravel News's $500 sale-price sponsored post. Large AI newsletters start at roughly $1,800–$2,500 per send.
- **Comparability limits.** Newsletter subscribers ≠ opens ≠ page views ≠ sponsor-card impressions ≠ podcast listens.
  - Opens are inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection; Cooperpress itself notes this.
  - A card may sit below the fold, so viewable impressions are fewer than page views.
  - 26,800 monthly visitors are not unique per send; a newsletter send reaches each subscriber once.
  - Monthly card pricing vs per-send pricing needs normalizing: a weekly newsletter sells about 4.3 sends/month, so a $1,800/issue newsletter costs about $7,700/month for continuous presence.

## Q2. Published click-through rates and typical clicks per placement

### Takeaway
Newsletters publish click figures far more often than display networks do. The Rundown claims 2,000–4,000 clicks per main ad (on ~0.9–1M impressions) and a 2.5% click rate; This Week in React claims an 11% click rate. Carbon claims a 0.8–2.1% CTR. EthicalAds publishes only a publisher-side CTR floor of 0.1%. I found no TLDR click guarantee.

### Inferences
- **Clicks ClaudeCodeHQ could expect.** Applying the published display CTR range (0.1% EthicalAds floor to 2.1% Carbon top) to 49,100 monthly page views gives roughly **49–1,030 clicks/month** for a site-wide card. ## Q4. Contract and packaging norms (multi-week discounts, bundles, minimums, lead times)

### Takeaway
The market norms are:
- multi-placement discounts of 5–20%;
- minimum commitments of 4 weeks (podcasts, small newsletters), 3 months (Stack Overflow) or 4+ months (Pragmatic Engineer podcast);
- spend-tier discounts for ad networks;
- prepayment.

Rate cards for big publishers are quarterly (EthicalAds, Cooperpress) and increasingly quote-only.

### Cited Findings
- **Ben's Bites**: 5% off for 2 slots, 10% for 3, 20% for 4+. — [ads.bensbites.com](https://ads.bensbites.com/)
- **Superhuman AI**: 15% discount for a 4-issue purchase ($8,499). — [Passionfroot: Zain Kahn](https://www.passionfroot.me/zain-kahn)
- **Changelog**: 4-week minimum at $2,500/week. — [Changelog sponsorship pricing](https://changelog.com/sponsor/pricing)
- **DevOps'ish**: 4-week minimum at $750/week. — [DevOps'ish sponsor](https://buttondown.com/devopsish/archive/sponsor)
- **The Pragmatic Engineer podcast**: long-term sponsors only, 4+ months. — [Podcast sponsorship](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/podcast-sponsorship/)
- **EthicalAds**: $1,000 minimum; 10% / 15% spend-tier discounts at $3k / $25k; +10% for auto-renew; quarterly price updates. — [EthicalAds pricing](https://www.ethicalads.io/advertisers/pricing/)
- **Carbon**: prepay to hold inventory; ~1M impressions/month recommended minimum; up to 15% added value for commitments; $5,000/month minimum for the Carbon Cover unit. — [Carbon Ads more-info](https://carbonads.net/more-info)
- **Stack Overflow**: $15,000 for 1 month or $10,000/month for 3 months, which rewards longer commitments with a lower monthly floor. — [Stack Overflow advertising policy](https://stackoverflow.co/advertising/policies/)
- **This Week in React**:
  - Most popular package: 4 "first sponsor" slots. — [thisweekinreact.com/sponsor](https://thisweekinreact.com/sponsor)
  - Historic bundle pricing: 200€ ×1, 400€ ×4, 1,000€ ×12. — [slorber gist](https://gist.github.com/slorber/cb732fb5d0a002c4d73236a9baeba7bb)
- **Next.js Weekly**: 1 issue €160, 4 issues €500, 8 issues €900 (≈22% and 30% per-issue discounts, my calculation). — [Next.js Weekly sponsors](https://nextjsweekly.com/sponsors)
- **Exclusivity**: one sponsor per issue at PyCoder's Weekly ([Real Python media kit 2023 Q4](https://static.realpython.com/realpython-media-kit-2023-q4.pdf)); one main ad plus one tool slot per send at Ben's Bites ([ads.bensbites.com](https://ads.bensbites.com/)); one spot per section per issue at Superhuman ([Passionfroot](https://www.passionfroot.me/zain-kahn)).
- **Sell-out signals**: PyCoder's Weekly noted ad slots "starting to book up". — [pycoders.com issue](https://pycoders.com/issue/299)
- **Rate increases and pricing on request**:
  - This Week in React raised prices in April 2024 due to demand for the first slot. — [slorber gist](https://gist.github.com/slorber/cb732fb5d0a002c4d73236a9baeba7bb)
  - A 2026 buyer's guide notes most larger newsletters now share pricing on request. — [daily.dev best newsletters to sponsor](https://business.daily.dev/resources/best-developer-newsletters-to-sponsor/)
- **Services included**: TLDR builds custom proposals and includes copywriting and campaign reports. — [TLDR how to advertise to developers](https://advertise.tldr.tech/how-to-advertise-to-developers/)

### Inferences
- ClaudeCodeHQ's monthly card pricing fits market norms. Common add-ons would be:
  - a 3-month commitment discount of ~10–15% (cf. EthicalAds, Stack Overflow, Superhuman);
  - a "category + site-wide" bundle;
  - explicit one-sponsor-per-category exclusivity, which matches newsletter primary-slot exclusivity and is the main differentiator vs rotating ad networks;
  - monthly click/CTR reporting.
- Publishing a transparent rate card is itself a differentiator now that the large AI newsletters are quote-only.

### Gaps
- No published sell-out lead times (e.g. "booked 6–8 weeks out") were found for TLDR, Cooperpress or Ben's Bites.
- No Passionfroot or Paved marketplace-wide AI/dev rate data was found; only per-creator storefronts and generic guides.
