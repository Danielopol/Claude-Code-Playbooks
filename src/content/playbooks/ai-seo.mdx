## What This Does

Helps you make your content discoverable, extractable, and citable by AI systems including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot. Audits your current AI visibility, identifies citation gaps versus competitors, and produces a structure-authority-presence optimization plan grounded in the Princeton GEO research.

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Top 10–20 queries** that matter to your business
- **Current AI presence** — have you checked ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews?
- **Content types** — blog, docs, comparison pages, product pages
- **Competitive landscape** — who is being cited where you're not
- **Existing structured data** (schema markup) on your site

## How It Works

1. **Audit AI visibility** — test 10–20 priority queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews; record who is cited
2. **Check content extractability** — clear definitions, self-contained answer blocks, statistics with sources, FAQ structure, schema markup, freshness signals
3. **Verify AI bot access** — ensure GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, and Bingbot are allowed in robots.txt
4. **Apply the three pillars** — Structure (extractable blocks), Authority (citations, statistics, expert quotes), Presence (Wikipedia, Reddit, review sites, YouTube)
5. **Add machine-readable files** — `/pricing.md` and `/llms.txt` so AI agents can parse your offering without rendering JavaScript
6. **Implement schema markup** — Article, FAQPage, HowTo, Product, ItemList for comparisons
7. **Set up monitoring** — track AI Overview presence, brand citation rate, and share of AI voice month-over-month

## What You Get

- AI visibility audit with query-by-query citation status across major AI platforms
- Content extractability checklist per priority page
- robots.txt and AI bot access review
- Three-pillar optimization plan (Structure, Authority, Presence)
- Schema markup recommendations matched to content types
- `/pricing.md` and `/llms.txt` templates for AI agents
- Monitoring plan with metrics and tools (Otterly, Peec AI, ZipTie)

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Cite sources, add statistics, add quotations** — Princeton GEO research shows these boost AI citation by 30–40%
- **Keyword stuffing actively hurts AI visibility** (-10%) — unlike traditional SEO where it's just ineffective
- **Third-party presence often matters more than your own site** — Wikipedia mentions account for ~7.8% of all ChatGPT citations
- **Comparison articles get ~33% of AI citations** — prioritize "[X] vs [Y]" and "Best [X] alternatives" content
- **Show "Last updated" dates prominently** — undated content loses to dated content because AI weights recency heavily
- **Don't gate authoritative content** — AI can't access what's behind a form
