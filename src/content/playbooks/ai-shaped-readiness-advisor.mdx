## What This Does

Assesses whether your product work is "AI-first" (using AI to automate existing tasks faster) or "AI-shaped" (fundamentally redesigning how product teams operate around AI capabilities). Scores five PM competencies at Level 1-4, identifies the priority gap, and produces a week-by-week action plan to close it.

The key distinction: if a competitor can replicate your AI usage by throwing bodies at it, it's not differentiation — it's efficiency, which becomes table stakes within months.

---

## The 5 Competencies

1. **Context Design** — Building a durable "reality layer" both humans and AI can trust (constraints registry, operational glossary, memory architecture)
2. **Agent Orchestration** — Repeatable, traceable AI workflows instead of one-off prompts
3. **Outcome Acceleration** — Using AI to compress learning cycles (not just speed up tasks)
4. **Team-AI Facilitation** — Redesigning team systems so AI operates as co-intelligence
5. **Strategic Differentiation** — Creating defensible competitive advantages competitors can't replicate

---

## Priority Logic

- **Context Design is foundational.** If Level 1-2, this is priority #1.
- **Agent Orchestration enables Outcome Acceleration.** Focus here once context is solid.
- **Team-AI Facilitation is parallel** — required for scale.
- **Strategic Differentiation requires Levels 3+ on others.** Don't focus here until the foundation is built.

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Mistaking efficiency for differentiation ("2x faster PRDs" is table stakes)
- Skipping Context Design and building fragile workflows on top of vibes
- Individual AI usage without team transformation — can't scale when you're on vacation
- Focusing on tools (Claude vs. ChatGPT) instead of workflows
- Speed over learning — shipping the wrong thing faster if validation cycles aren't compressed
