## What This Does

Analyze App Store and Google Play listings against ASO best practices. Fetches live listing data, scores metadata, visuals, and ratings, then produces a prioritized action plan with rewritten metadata that fits within character limits.

## Inputs You'll Need

- **App Store or Google Play URL** for the app being audited
- **Target audience and category** for the app
- **Top competitors** (URLs) for keyword gap analysis
- **Goals** — visibility, conversion, both

## How It Works

1. Fetch the live listing data (title, subtitle, short/long description, keyword field, screenshots, ratings, reviews)
2. Score each metadata field against character limits and keyword density best practices
3. Mine the keyword field for duplicates, low-volume terms, and missed opportunities
4. Compare against top 3–5 competitors to find keyword gaps
5. Audit visual assets (icon, screenshots, preview video) for conversion impact
6. Review ratings and reviews for sentiment and recurring complaints
7. Produce a prioritized action plan — quick wins first, then structural changes

## What You Get

- Listing scorecard for iOS and Google Play with pass/fail per field
- Character-perfect rewrites for title, subtitle, short description, full description, and keyword field
- Competitor keyword gap analysis
- Screenshot and visual asset recommendations
- A/B testing plan (icon, screenshots, title, short description)
- Localization opportunities for top markets

## Tips & Best Practices

- **iOS keyword field**: use commas, no spaces, no duplicates of title/subtitle words (Apple indexes them automatically)
- **Google Play indexes the description** — keyword density in the long description matters
- **Front-load your title** — the first 20 characters appear in search results
- **Screenshots aren't indexed but drive conversion** — treat them as ad creative
- **Update with major releases** — algorithm freshness signals matter
