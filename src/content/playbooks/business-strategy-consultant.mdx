## What This Does

A complete business strategy toolkit that replaces expensive consultants. Includes 10 strategic frameworks: SWOT analysis, growth levers, 30-60-90 plans, revenue models, churn reduction, KPI dashboards, pricing strategy, go-to-market plans, value propositions, and pivot strategies.

Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.


## Example Output

```markdown
## SWOT Analysis: CloudTask (Project Management SaaS)

### Executive Summary
- Strong product-market fit in SMB segment
- Vulnerable to enterprise competitors moving downmarket
- Key opportunity: AI-powered automation features

### Strengths
- Fast onboarding (avg 15 min to first project)
- 92% customer satisfaction score
- 40% lower price than main competitors
- Strong integrations ecosystem

### Weaknesses
- No enterprise features (SSO, audit logs)
- Limited brand awareness
- Small sales team (3 reps)
- No mobile app

### Opportunities
- AI automation demand growing 300% YoY
- Competitors neglecting 10-50 employee segment
- Remote work tools consolidation trend
- Partnership opportunities with accounting software

### Threats
- Asana/Monday moving to freemium
- Economic downturn reducing software budgets
- Feature commoditization in space
- Key engineer poaching by FAANG

### Recommendations
1. **Immediate**: Launch AI task automation (3 months)
2. **Near-term**: Build mobile app (6 months)
3. **Strategic**: Partner with QuickBooks/Xero
```

## Tips

- **Be specific**: "B2B SaaS in healthcare" works better than "software company"
- **Provide context**: Share revenue, team size, current challenges
- **Iterate**: Use one framework's output as input for another
- **Combine frameworks**: SWOT → Growth Levers → GTM is powerful
- **Challenge assumptions**: Ask Claude to stress-test conclusions

## Troubleshooting

**Analysis too generic**
Provide more specifics: industry, company size, target customer, unique constraints

**Need competitive data**
Ask Claude to research specific competitors first, then run analysis

**Recommendations not actionable**
Ask: "Break down recommendation #1 into specific tasks with owners"
