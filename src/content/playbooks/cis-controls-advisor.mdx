## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into a CIS Controls v8 (CIS Top 18) advisor. It scopes Implementation Groups (IG1/IG2/IG3), runs safeguard-level gap assessments, gives implementation guidance across all 18 controls — from asset and software inventory through access control, vulnerability management, audit logging, malware defenses, incident response, and penetration testing — and maps CIS Controls to NIST CSF, ISO 27001, SOC 2, and CMMC.

---

## The Problem

Most teams know they need to "do security" but drown in frameworks. The CIS Controls solve the prioritization problem with Implementation Groups and concrete safeguards — but you still have to pick the right IG for your size and risk, assess honestly at the safeguard level, and sequence the work so the highest-value cyber hygiene happens first.

## Tips

- **Pick the right IG** — IG1 is essential hygiene; don't over-scope a small team.
- **Start with inventory** — Controls 1 and 2 enable everything else.
- **Use the mappings** — CIS work counts toward NIST CSF, ISO 27001, and more.

## Important Disclaimer

This is a security planning and assessment tool, not a certification. Have qualified security professionals review scoping and assessments before relying on them.
