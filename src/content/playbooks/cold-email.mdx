## What This Does

Write cold outreach that reads like a sharp human, not a template. Covers subject lines, opening lines, body copy, CTAs, personalization that connects to the problem, and multi-touch follow-up sequences with rotating angles.

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Who you're writing to** — role, company, why them specifically
- **What you want** — the outcome (meeting, reply, intro, demo)
- **Your value** — the specific problem you solve for people like them
- **Your proof** — a result, case study, or credibility signal
- **Research signals** — funding, hiring, LinkedIn posts, news, tech stack changes

## How It Works

1. Calibrate voice to audience (C-suite ultra-brief, mid-level more specific, technical precise)
2. Pick a structure that fits — Observation → Problem → Proof → Ask, or Trigger → Insight → Ask
3. Write a subject line that looks internal: 2–4 lowercase words, no urgency tricks
4. Personalize at a level connected to the problem (Level 1–4)
5. Cut every sentence that doesn't move the reader toward replying
6. Design 3–5 follow-ups with rotating angles, increasing gaps, and a real breakup email
7. Quality-check by reading aloud and asking "would I reply to this?"

## What You Get

- Email drafts (initial + follow-ups) calibrated to your voice and audience
- Subject line variations
- Personalization research per prospect
- Cadence plan with timing and angles
- Breakup email that's honest, not passive-aggressive

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Lead with their world, not yours** — "you/your" should dominate over "I/we"
- **One ask, low friction** — interest-based CTAs ("Worth exploring?") beat meeting requests
- **Avoid the AI tells** — no "I hope this email finds you well," no "leverage," no "best-in-class"
- **Personalization must connect to the problem** — if removing the personalization still makes sense, it isn't working
- **Each follow-up adds something new** — "just checking in" gives the reader nothing to respond to
