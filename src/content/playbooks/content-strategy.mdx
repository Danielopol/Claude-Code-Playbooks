## What This Does

Plans content that is searchable, shareable, or both — turning vague "we should blog more" energy into a structured strategy with topic clusters, an editorial calendar, and goals tied to traffic, authority, and leads.

---

### Step 1: Create a Project Folder

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above and save the file as `CLAUDE.md` in that folder.

### Step 3: Run the Workflow

Describe your business, audience, and content goals.

---

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Business context** — what the company does, ICP, primary content goal (traffic, leads, brand)
- **Customer research** — pain points, language, buying triggers
- **Existing content** — what's published, what works, what doesn't
- **Resources** — writing capacity, in-house expertise, budget
- **Distribution channels** — where you can amplify

## How It Works

1. Map customer journey to content stages (awareness, evaluation, decision)
2. Identify content pillars and topic clusters
3. Mix searchable content (SEO) and shareable content (POV/expert)
4. Prioritize by impact + effort
5. Build editorial calendar with goals per piece
6. Define distribution and repurposing plan
7. Set success metrics per content type

## What You Get

- Content strategy document with pillars, clusters, and rationale
- 90-day editorial calendar with topics, formats, owners, and KPIs
- Keyword and topic research per cluster
- Distribution and repurposing plan
- Measurement framework with leading and lagging indicators
