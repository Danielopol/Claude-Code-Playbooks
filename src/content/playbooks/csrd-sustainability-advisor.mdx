## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into a CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive, EU 2022/2464) advisor. It covers scope and threshold analysis, double materiality assessment, the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS E1-E5 environmental, S1-S4 social, G1 governance), value-chain reporting, XBRL digital tagging, third-party assurance, implementation timelines, gap assessments, and alignment with GRI, TCFD, and SASB.

---

## The Problem

CSRD is a step-change from voluntary ESG reporting: mandatory, standardized ESRS disclosures, audited assurance, machine-readable XBRL tagging, and — hardest of all — a double materiality assessment that determines what you even have to report. Companies with mature sustainability narratives still find the rigor, structure, and value-chain reach of CSRD daunting.

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create Your Workspace

```bash
mkdir -p ~/Documents/CSRD
```

### Step 2: Download the Template

```bash
mv ~/Downloads/CLAUDE.md ~/Documents/CSRD/
```

### Step 3: Add Context (Optional)

Describe your company size, sector, value chain, and any existing sustainability reporting (GRI/TCFD/SASB).

### Step 4: Run Claude Code

```bash
cd ~/Documents/CSRD
claude
```

### Step 5: Start

Say: **"Run a CSRD scoping check and structure our double materiality assessment."**

## Tips

- **Start with double materiality** — it scopes everything you report.
- **Plan value-chain data early** — it's often the hardest to collect.
- **Design for XBRL and assurance** — the report must be tagged and auditable.

## Important Disclaimer

This is a reporting-support tool, not assurance or legal advice. CSRD reports require third-party assurance and must meet ESRS and member-state requirements. Have qualified sustainability and audit professionals review outputs before relying on them.
