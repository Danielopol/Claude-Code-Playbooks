## What This Does

Facilitates a 5-question adaptive workshop that builds a customer journey map from scratch — actor (persona), scenario/goal, 4-6 journey phases, per-phase actions/thoughts/emotions/pain-points, and 5-7 opportunities ranked by impact with supporting evidence.

Built on NNGroup's framework. Pairs with `workshop-facilitation` for one-question-at-a-time interaction, numbered options, and pause/resume.

## Quick Start

```bash
mkdir -p ~/Documents/JourneyWorkshop
mv ~/Downloads/CLAUDE.md ~/Documents/JourneyWorkshop/
cd ~/Documents/JourneyWorkshop

```

Paste your context (interviews, support tickets, analytics, personas) and describe the customer experience. Claude runs the 5 questions and delivers the full map.

---

## Five Key Components

1. **Actor** — A specific persona anchoring the map
2. **Scenario + Expectations** — Situational context and goals
3. **Journey Phases** — 4-6 high-level stages (discover, try, buy, use, support)
4. **Actions, Mindsets, Emotions** — User behaviors, thoughts, feelings per phase
5. **Opportunities** — Prioritized improvement insights with evidence

---

## What This Is NOT

- **Not a service blueprint** — Journey maps are customer-perspective; blueprints map internal operations
- **Not a user story map** — Journey maps support discovery; story maps support implementation
- **Not an experience map** — Journey maps target specific users and products; experience maps explore broader human behaviors

---

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Gather context first.** Paste interviews, churn data, analytics, NPS — the richer the input, the sharper the map.
- **Map from customer POV.** Not "lead → qualified → demo → closed" (that's your sales funnel). Use "discovers problem → researches → tries → adopts."
- **One persona per map.** If you have multiple personas, create separate maps — don't merge.
- **Rank opportunities by evidence.** HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW based on frequency, drop-off data, and emotional intensity — not gut feel.
- **Facilitate cross-functionally.** PM + design + engineering + support together builds shared mental model.

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Mapping sales process instead of customer experience
- Actions only, no emotions or pain points — misses where experience breaks
- Too many personas in one map — becomes generic
- 20 unranked opportunities → paralysis; rank by impact with evidence
- PM creates map solo → no shared understanding → map doesn't drive decisions
