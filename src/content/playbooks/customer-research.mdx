## What This Does

Helps uncover what customers actually think, feel, say, and struggle with — so positioning, product, and copy are grounded in reality rather than assumption. Works in two modes: analyzing existing research assets (transcripts, surveys, reviews, tickets) and going to find research from online sources (Reddit, G2, forums).

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Mode** — analyze existing assets, or go find research online
- **Source materials** — transcripts, surveys, reviews, support tickets, sales call notes
- **Research goal** — positioning, copy input, persona development, JTBD, churn diagnosis
- **Online sources** — Reddit, G2, Capterra, forums, communities, YouTube comments

## How It Works

1. **Mode 1 (analyze):** code transcripts/reviews for themes, pain points, language patterns, and triggers
2. **Mode 2 (gather):** map digital watering holes, search for relevant threads, mine quotes
3. Synthesize findings into VOC themes, JTBD statements, or persona drafts
4. Pull direct quotes for use in copy and positioning
5. Distinguish signal from noise (recurring patterns vs one-off complaints)
6. Tie findings to actions (copy changes, product priorities, retention plays)

## What You Get

- Voice-of-customer themes with frequency and quotes
- JTBD synthesis or persona drafts
- Pain point and trigger map
- Language pattern library (words customers actually use)
- Action recommendations tied to copy, product, or retention
