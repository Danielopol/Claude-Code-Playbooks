## What This Does

Builds a compounding backlink and discovery foundation by submitting to the right directories in the right order with the right positioning — covering startup, SaaS, AI, agent, MCP, no-code, and review directories — so the foundation produces leads instead of vanity backlinks.

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Product** — what it does, category, target market
- **Stage** — pre-launch, launching, post-launch, ongoing
- **Existing listings** — where you're already listed
- **Goals** — backlinks, DR, discovery, AI citation, launch buzz

## How It Works

1. Categorize directories by type (general SaaS, AI/agents, MCP, no-code, review sites, niche)
2. Score each by DR, traffic, and AI citation likelihood
3. Sequence submissions (high-effort, high-reward first; quick wins in parallel)
4. Write per-directory positioning and asset packs
5. Track submission, approval, and listing freshness
6. Plan ongoing maintenance (refresh quarterly, monitor reviews)

## What You Get

- Tier-ranked directory list with priority order
- Per-directory tagline, description, screenshots, and category placement
- Submission tracker (status, date, approval, link)
- Follow-up plan for review collection and listing updates
- Notes on which directories drive AI citations

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Foundation, not whole strategy** — directories don't replace SEO, content, or paid
- **AI engines pull from high-DR directories** — these increasingly drive AI-mediated discovery
- **Refresh listings quarterly** — stale screenshots and pricing erode trust
- **Reviews on G2/Capterra compound over time** — set up an ongoing collection motion
