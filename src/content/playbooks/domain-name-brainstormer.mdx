## What This Does

Generate creative domain name ideas for your projects, products, or brands. Claude analyzes your concept, creates relevant naming options, and checks availability across multiple TLDs.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Start Claude

```bash
claude
```

### Step 2: Describe Your Project

Tell Claude about your project, brand, or idea:

**"I need domain name ideas for a project management tool for remote teams"**

## TLD Recommendations

| TLD | Best For | Price Range |
|-----|----------|-------------|
| .com | Universal trust, businesses | $10-15/year |
| .io | Tech startups, SaaS | $30-50/year |
| .dev | Developer tools | $15-20/year |
| .ai | AI/ML products | $50-100/year |
| .app | Mobile/web apps | $15-20/year |

## Good Domain Characteristics

- **Short**: Under 15 characters ideal
- **Memorable**: Easy to recall
- **Pronounceable**: Can be said aloud
- **Descriptive**: Hints at what you do
- **Brandable**: Unique and ownable
- **No hyphens**: Easier to communicate

## What You'll Get

For each suggestion:
- Domain name idea
- Available TLDs
- Reasoning for the name
- Alternative variations
- Pricing context

---

## Additional Checks

After finding a domain, also verify:
- Social media handle availability
- Trademark conflicts
- Pronunciation in different languages
- Potential negative meanings

---

## Tips

- **Register quickly**: Good domains get taken fast
- **Protect your brand**: Get multiple TLDs
- **Avoid numbers**: Harder to communicate verbally
- **Test pronunciation**: Say it aloud to others
- **Check trademarks**: Avoid legal issues
- **Think long-term**: Will the name still fit in 5 years?
