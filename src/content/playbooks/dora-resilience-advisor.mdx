## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into a DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554) advisor for EU financial entities. It covers ICT risk management frameworks (Art. 6), ICT incident classification and reporting (Art. 17), threat-led penetration testing (Art. 26 TLPT), ICT third-party risk management and contractual provisions (Arts. 28-30), the Register of Information (CIR 2024/2956), ICT concentration risk, oversight of critical ICT third-party providers, the simplified framework, gap analysis, and DORA-vs-NIS2 comparison.

---

## The Problem

DORA is broad and prescriptive: a formal ICT risk framework, classified incident reporting on regulatory timelines, periodic threat-led penetration testing, and rigorous third-party risk management — including a Register of Information covering every ICT provider and specific contractual clauses. Financial entities that treated resilience informally now have to evidence it against RTS/ITS detail.

### Step 1: Create Your Workspace

```bash
mkdir -p ~/Documents/DORA
```

### Step 2: Download the Template

```bash
mv ~/Downloads/CLAUDE.md ~/Documents/DORA/
```

### Step 3: Add Context (Optional)

Add your ICT vendor list, incident process, and existing resilience or risk documentation.

### Step 4: Run Claude Code

```bash

```

### Step 5: Start

Say: **"Run a DORA gap analysis and outline our ICT risk management framework."**

## Tips

- **Build the Register early** — it underpins third-party risk and oversight.
- **Operationalize incident classification** — reporting clocks start fast.
- **Scope TLPT** — only some entities must perform it; confirm whether you do.
