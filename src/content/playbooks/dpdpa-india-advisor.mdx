## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into an advisor on India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the DPDP Rules 2025. It covers Data Fiduciary obligations, Data Principal rights, consent and notice requirements (Section 6), legitimate uses (Section 7), children's data (Section 9), Significant Data Fiduciary duties (Section 10 / Rule 13), cross-border transfers (Section 16), breach notification (Rule 6), gap analysis, a compliance roadmap, and DPDPA-vs-GDPR comparison.

---

## The Problem

The DPDPA introduces a consent-first regime with verifiable parental consent for children, Significant Data Fiduciary obligations, and India-specific breach and transfer rules — all now being operationalized through the 2025 Rules and a new Data Protection Board. Global companies that built for GDPR still have gaps: India's notice format, consent-manager expectations, children's-data rules, and SDF duties don't map one-to-one.

## Tips

- **Decide your SDF status early** — it changes DPIA, audit, and DPO obligations.
- **Treat consent as a product surface** — notice format and consent managers matter under the Rules.
- **Map transfers explicitly** — India's approach differs from GDPR adequacy.
