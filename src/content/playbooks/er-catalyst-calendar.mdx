## What This Does

Creates and maintains a structured calendar of upcoming catalysts — earnings dates, conferences, product launches, regulatory decisions, and macro events — across your entire coverage universe. Generates weekly previews with position implications and risk management notes.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create a Project Folder

Create a folder for your research workspace and place the downloaded template inside as `CLAUDE.md`.

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above, then move the file into your project folder as `CLAUDE.md`.

### Step 3: Start Working

```
"Build a catalyst calendar for AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN for the next month"
"What events could move my positions this week?"
"Generate a weekly preview for my coverage universe"
"Add the Fed meeting and CPI release to the macro events"
```

## Event Types Tracked

- **Earnings & Financial**: Quarterly reports, investor days, capital markets days, debt maturities
- **Corporate**: Product launches, FDA approvals, M&A milestones, management transitions
- **Industry**: Conferences, trade shows, regulatory rulings, industry data releases
- **Macro**: FOMC meetings, jobs reports, CPI/GDP releases, central bank decisions

## Best Practices

- Verify earnings dates against company IR pages closer to the date — they shift frequently
- Track pre-announce risk for companies with a history of issuing warnings
- Color-code by impact level: Red = high impact, Yellow = moderate, Green = routine
- Archive past catalysts with actual outcomes to build pattern recognition over time
- Conference attendance lists reveal which companies are presenting — and which are conspicuously absent
