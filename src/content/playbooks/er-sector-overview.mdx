## What This Does

Creates comprehensive industry and sector landscape reports covering market size, growth drivers, competitive positioning, company profiles with valuation snapshots, and investment implications. Supports both high-level overviews (5-10 pages) and deep dives (20-30 pages).

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create a Project Folder

Create a folder for your sector research and place the downloaded template inside as `CLAUDE.md`.

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above, then move the file into your project folder as `CLAUDE.md`.

### Step 3: Start Working

```
"Create a sector overview of cloud infrastructure"
"Industry deep dive on the semiconductor supply chain"
"Market landscape for fintech payments"
"Thematic research on AI infrastructure buildout"
```

## Report Components

- **Market Size & Growth**: TAM, historical CAGR, forecast growth, segmentation
- **Industry Structure**: Fragmentation, value chain, business models, barriers to entry
- **Competitive Landscape**: Top 5-10 players with valuation snapshots and moat analysis
- **Valuation Context**: Current vs. historical multiples, M&A premiums
- **Investment Implications**: Best risk/reward opportunities and key debates

## Best Practices

- Source all market size data — cite the research firm or methodology
- Distinguish between TAM hype and realistic addressable market
- Sector overviews age fast — date-stamp everything and flag potentially stale data
- Charts are essential — market size waterfall, competitive positioning matrix, valuation scatter plot
