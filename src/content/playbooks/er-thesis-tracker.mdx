## What This Does

Maintains structured investment theses for portfolio positions and watchlist names. Tracks key pillars, logs new data points with thesis impact assessments, maintains catalyst calendars, and monitors conviction levels over time. Designed for morning meetings, portfolio reviews, and risk committee presentations.

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create a Project Folder

Create a folder for your thesis tracking workspace and place the downloaded template inside as `CLAUDE.md`.

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above, then move the file into your project folder as `CLAUDE.md`.

### Step 3: Start Working

```
"Create a new thesis for NVDA — long on AI infrastructure spending"
"Update my AAPL thesis with Q3 earnings data"
"Is my short thesis on XYZ still intact?"
"Do a full portfolio thesis review"
"Add the FDA decision catalyst to my biotech thesis"
```

## Thesis Components

- **Thesis statement**: 1-2 sentence core thesis
- **Key pillars**: 3-5 supporting arguments with status tracking
- **Key risks**: 3-5 invalidation triggers
- **Catalyst calendar**: Upcoming events that prove/disprove the thesis
- **Update log**: Chronological data points with impact assessment
- **Conviction level**: High / Medium / Low with trend

## Best Practices

- A thesis should be falsifiable — if nothing could disprove it, it's not a thesis
- Track disconfirming evidence as rigorously as confirming evidence
- Review theses at least quarterly, even when nothing dramatic has happened
- Store thesis data in a structured format for cross-session reference
