## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into an EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) advisor for products with digital elements (PDEs) sold in the EU. It handles gap analysis, product classification (Default / Class I / Class II), conformity-assessment route selection, CE marking, SBOM requirements, vulnerability and incident reporting to ENISA/CSIRTs, support-period obligations, and the duties of manufacturers, importers, and distributors.

---

## The Problem

The CRA brings mandatory cybersecurity to virtually every connected or software product on the EU market. Manufacturers must meet Annex I essential requirements, classify their product correctly, choose a conformity-assessment route, produce an SBOM, handle vulnerabilities, and report incidents — while importers and distributors carry their own checks. Many product teams have never operated under a CE-marking regime for security.

## Tips

- **Classify the product first** — it sets the conformity route.
- **Stand up vulnerability handling now** — it's an ongoing obligation, not a one-time check.
- **Plan the support period** — security updates are a defined duty.
