## What This Does

Builds and populates integrated 3-statement financial models (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement) with proper cross-statement linkages, scenario analysis, and professional formatting. The model includes supporting schedules for debt, working capital, and depreciation, plus a ratios dashboard and scenario toggle.

## Model Architecture

The model produces a 10-tab Excel workbook: Cover, Assumptions, Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, Debt Schedule, Working Capital, Depreciation & Amortization, Ratios & Output, and Scenarios. All tabs are interconnected with formula-driven linkages.

**Critical linkage points:**
- Net Income flows from IS to BS (Retained Earnings) and to CFS (top line)
- D&A flows to IS (expense) and CFS (add-back)
- CapEx flows from CFS to BS (PP&E rollforward)
- Working Capital changes flow from BS to CFS
- Debt issuance/repayment flows from Debt Schedule to CFS and BS
- Cash ending balance on CFS must equal Cash on BS

## Formatting Standards

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Blue font | Hard-coded input / assumption |
| Black font | Formula / calculated cell |
| Green font | Link to another tab |
| Red font | Error check or warning |

## Validation Checks

The model includes built-in validation: BS balance check (must equal zero), cash tie-out between CFS and BS, retained earnings rollforward, debt schedule ties, D&A ties, working capital ties, and reasonableness checks on tax rates, revenue growth, and margins.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Always place assumptions in blue font on a dedicated Assumptions tab — no hard-coded numbers in formula cells
- Use a scenario toggle (1 = Base, 2 = Upside, 3 = Downside) with CHOOSE or INDEX functions
- Place the Balance Check prominently with conditional formatting (green if zero, red if non-zero)
- For the debt schedule, use beginning balance for interest calculations to avoid circular references
- Historical years go on the left, projected years on the right, with a clear separator between them
