## What This Does

Builds institutional-grade comparable company analyses in Excel with a complete workflow: peer group selection, market data gathering, operating metrics, growth and margin analysis, valuation multiples (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E, and more), statistical analysis with quartiles, implied valuation bridging, and a summary football field chart.

## Output Sections

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Header | Company names, tickers, share prices, dates |
| Market Data | Diluted shares, market cap, EV bridge |
| Operating Metrics | Revenue, EBITDA, EBIT, Net Income (historical + projected) |
| Growth & Margins | Revenue growth, EBITDA growth, gross/EBITDA/operating margins |
| Valuation Multiples | EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E, EV/EBIT (LTM + NTM) |
| Statistics | Mean, Median, 25th/75th percentile, High, Low |
| Implied Valuation | Apply peer multiples to target for EV-to-equity bridge |

## Industry-Specific Metrics

The template includes guidance for industry-specific multiples: SaaS (EV/ARR, Rule of 40, NRR), Banks (P/TBV, ROE, NIM), REITs (P/FFO, Cap Rate), Retail (EV/Store, Same-Store Sales), Healthcare (EV/Subscriber), and more.

## Quality Checklist

- All share prices as of the same date
- Diluted share counts use treasury stock method consistently
- Enterprise value calculation includes all components (Debt, Cash, Preferred, Minority Interest)
- EBITDA adjustments applied consistently across all comps
- NM (not meaningful) shown for negative denominators instead of misleading numbers
- Implied valuation bridges correctly from EV to equity value to per-share

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Select 8-15 comparable companies** based on industry, business model, size, geography, and growth profile
- **Document inclusion/exclusion rationale** for each peer
- **Exclude outliers** from statistics (multiples > 2 standard deviations from mean) but keep them in the table with a footnote
- **Use median over mean** — it is less sensitive to outliers
- **Calculate multiples for LTM and NTM** to show both historical and forward-looking valuations
