## What This Does

Documents a reference website's visual system so you can recreate its style or capture its UI patterns. The output is a `DESIGN.md`.

The process:

1. **Find the site.** It starts with your URL and requested pages. If you only name an organization, it resolves the official site first, and asks if the reference is ambiguous.
2. **Capture a representative page** with `firecrawl_scrape`, requesting branding, HTML, markdown, and screenshot formats (with a full-page screenshot when supported, and `onlyMainContent: false`). It adds images only when that format is exposed, and fetches extra pages only for your requested scope or a material missing pattern.
3. **Look at the screenshot pixels** before describing the layout. A screenshot URL alone isn't inspected evidence. HTML and stylesheet evidence fill gaps in fonts, tokens, and components.
4. **Extract** semantic color roles, typography, spacing, containers, borders, shadows, component patterns, section order, and imagery treatment, with source evidence attached to material values.
5. **Write `DESIGN.md`** with source URLs and capture context, the reference screenshot, design tokens, layout and components, image references, implementation guidance for your stack, and unresolved details.

## Observed vs. Estimated

The template makes Claude label how sure it is:

- **Extracted values** come straight from branding data, HTML, or CSS.
- **Visual estimates** come from looking at the screenshot. Estimated spacing isn't presented as exact CSS.
- **Proposed implementation choices** are adaptations for your stack.

Differences between components, pages, and themes stay explicit. A desktop screenshot doesn't establish mobile layout, hover behavior, or animation, so Claude inspects extra viewports or states only when you need them. When it needs a fresh capture, it uses `maxAge: 0` and records the capture context.

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Say your target stack.** Implementation guidance is adapted to it.
- **Name the pages that matter.** One page rarely shows the whole system, but extra pages cost extra reads.
- **Supply your own assets.** Claude respects supplied assets and scope limits.
- **Use it for reference, not copying.** Recreating a visual style is different from copying a site's protected assets or content. Respect the source's terms and branding rights.

---

## Limitations

- **Not pixel-perfect.** Claude is told not to promise a pixel-perfect reconstruction or a complete asset capture.
- **Image inventories are partial.** Curated branding assets aren't a complete list of page images, and asset URLs aren't downloaded files.
- **One snapshot.** The result reflects the capture date. Record it and recapture if the site changes.
- **Hidden states aren't captured** unless inspected: hover, mobile, animation.
