## What This Does

Researches a fantasy decision (start/sit, waiver pickup, trade, draft pick, or keeper) from current public sources, then gives a clear recommendation.

The steps:

1. **Establish the decision.** Sport, scoring period, and the rules that could change the answer: your scoring settings, roster limits, available options, who you'd drop, league format, and costs or picks in a trade or keeper. Firecrawl supplies public research, not private roster access, so Claude doesn't assume a player is on your roster or available on your waiver wire. It asks only for missing context that matters.
2. **Check current evidence.** Team, opponent, schedule, injury or lineup status, and recent playing time or usage. It uses a fresh scrape for volatile status pages, and checks report timestamps against game time, because a successful fetch or undated page doesn't prove current status.
3. **Compare the options** under your scoring and roster constraints. Observed performance, sourced projections, and Claude's judgment are separated. Expert rankings for the right scoring format and period are a baseline with source dates, and consensus is established only from independent sources.
4. **Recommend.** The answer leads with a clear recommendation and concise cited reasons, names the assumption or unresolved status most likely to change the choice, and gives a usable alternative before lineup lock.

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Share your scoring and roster rules.** Format changes the answer, such as PPR vs. standard or superflex vs. single QB.
- **Say what's available.** Claude can't see your waiver wire, so list the options and who you'd drop.
- **Ask before lineup lock.** Injury and inactive news moves fast, and each source is dated.
- **Use the stated alternative.** The recommendation includes a fallback if the key status changes.
- **FAAB bids.** Claude gives a bid range only if your budget and league context support one, and labels it as judgment.

---

## Limitations

- **Read-only research.** It doesn't change rosters, submit claims, trades or lineups, place bets, or set up monitoring. It never claims a future recheck it won't make.
- **No private league access.** Unless you supply league details, recommendations are conditional on any facts it can't verify.
- **No invented numbers.** It won't make up point projections, win probabilities, or FAAB budgets.
- **Sports outcomes are uncertain.** This is research to inform a decision, not a prediction or betting advice.
