## What This Does

Discovers **new** professionals matching your criteria: prospects, experts, speakers, or candidates for outreach. For people you already have, use the Firecrawl lead enrichment playbook.

The workflow:

1. **Pin down the criteria.** Roles, company criteria, geography, and count. Claude separates required filters from preferences and distinguishes a person's location from company headquarters. It asks only for missing criteria that prevent a useful shortlist.
2. **Qualify companies first** when company criteria matter, then search for people within matching company domains. Named target companies are resolved directly.
3. **Search, preview, deduplicate, and rank** by evidence of fit. Claude enriches only shortlisted people whose requested fields are incomplete, resolves masked names before presenting identities, and follows provider pagination within your count and credit budget. It stops at sufficient verified results, exhausted cursors, or a stated coverage limit.
4. **Return a ranked table** with name, current title, company, company size when relevant, location, profile link, evidence of fit, and unmet or unknown criteria. Provider data is attributed, supporting pages are cited, and checked dates and freshness are shown.

For **experts and speakers**, Claude also uses publications, institutional bios, and past talks. A sales database alone doesn't establish expertise or speaking availability.

## Honest Lists

- **No silent relaxing.** Partial matches stay separate from verified matches. Claude never loosens a requirement to fill the count.
- **Shortfall stated.** The table is followed by how many distinct people and companies were returned and any gap against your target.
- **Conflicts shown.** Conflicting values appear separately with their sources.
- **Work email only on request.** And only when returned as an actual address, never an availability flag, unlock placeholder, or guess. Personal-email and phone-reveal options stay disabled.
- **An expert isn't a confirmed speaker.** Claude doesn't treat suitability as availability.

---

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Give hard criteria and nice-to-haves separately.** It makes ranking and shortfalls clearer.
- **Set a count and a credit budget.** Pagination stays inside them.
- **Qualify the company side first** for criteria like size, funding, or industry.
- **Verify before outreach.** Treat the list as research, and check anything critical.

---

## Limitations

- **Professional information only.** No sensitive traits, private personal details, CRM records, or outreach.
- **Provider coverage varies.** If provider access fails, Claude uses public professional sources within your allowed scope, or discloses the gaps.
- **Not exhaustive.** A ranked shortlist isn't every matching person.
- **Mind the rules.** Use of personal data and outreach are regulated in many places. Make sure your use complies.
