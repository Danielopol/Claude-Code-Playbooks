## What This Does

Gives you a ready-to-run plan for getting to your first 100 paying customers without relying on a launch, ads, or virality. The skill walks you through three concentric circles of sales:

- **Circle 1: Friends and Family** — start uncomfortable, sell to the people who trust you most
- **Circle 2: Your Community** — the people you've been contributing to
- **Circle 3: Strangers** — personalized cold outreach (how Gumroad actually grew)

Includes Sahil's original Gumroad cold email as a template.

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the template

Click **Download** above for the `CLAUDE.md` skill.

### Step 2: Drop it into your sales folder

Save as `CLAUDE.md` in the folder where you're tracking customer outreach.

### Step 3: Generate your outreach lists

Ask Claude: *"Help me sell to my first 100 customers. Here's my product: [describe]."* You'll get the 10+10 list, a cold email draft, a pricing recommendation, and a weekly tracking plan.

## Key Reframes

- **Sales isn't dirty** — you're helping people in your community, not convincing strangers
- **Manual sales = 99% of early growth.** Word of mouth = 99% of later growth.
- **You need fewer customers than you think** — Slack's IPO: 575 customers = 40% of revenue
- **Don't launch until you have 100 paying customers** — then launch as a celebration, not an acquisition play

## The Math

$10/month product × 200 customers = $2,000/month. At one customer per business day, that's under a year of manual selling.

## Cold Email Template (From the Skill)

> "Hi John, I saw you're selling a PDF on your website using PayPal, and manually emailing everyone who buys the PDF. I built a service called Gumroad which basically automates all of this. I'd love to show it to you, or you can check it out yourself: gumroad.com. Also happy to just share any learnings we see from creators in a little PDF we have. Let me know! Best, Sahil"

Don't copy-paste. Each email refines your ability to write better ones.

## Who Should Use This

Founders who've shipped a product but have zero customers, makers allergic to ads and growth hacking, anyone who needs a bootstrapped sales playbook that doesn't require a marketing budget.
