## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into an expert GDPR compliance assistant covering four core workflows: (1) auditing code and systems for GDPR violations, (2) drafting GDPR-compliant documents like privacy policies, Data Processing Agreements (DPAs), and consent notices, (3) answering GDPR questions with authoritative article citations, and (4) reviewing data flows and PII handling practices. Every answer ties back to specific GDPR articles and recitals, and flags items that need legal review.

---

## The Problem

GDPR touches almost every system that stores a name, email, or IP address — but compliance lives in the details. Engineers ship logging, analytics, and third-party integrations without knowing which lawful basis applies. Privacy policies are copied and never reflect actual processing. Consent banners fire trackers before the user clicks. Data-subject requests arrive and nobody knows where the data lives. By the time a regulator or enterprise customer asks, the gaps are expensive to close.

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create Your Workspace

```bash
mkdir -p ~/Documents/GDPR-Compliance
```

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above, then move `CLAUDE.md` into the folder:

```bash
mv ~/Downloads/CLAUDE.md ~/Documents/GDPR-Compliance/
```

### Step 3: Add Your Material (Optional)

Drop in source code, your current privacy policy, vendor agreements, or a data-flow description so the audit is grounded in your real setup.

### Step 4: Run Claude Code

```bash
cd ~/Documents/GDPR-Compliance
claude
```

### Step 5: Start

Say: **"Audit my application for GDPR compliance and list violations by article."**

## Tips

- **Give it real artifacts.** Pasted code, your actual policy, or a vendor list yields far sharper findings than abstract questions.
- **State your lawful bases.** Tell Claude whether you rely on consent, contract, or legitimate interest for each activity.
- **Layer in ePrivacy** for cookies and electronic communications alongside GDPR.
- **Re-audit on change.** Run it again whenever you add a vendor, SDK, or data flow.

## Important Disclaimer

This is a compliance drafting and review tool, not legal advice. GDPR involves legal judgments that vary by jurisdiction and processing activity. Have a qualified data protection lawyer or your DPO review outputs before relying on them. Fines can reach 4% of annual global turnover or €20 million.
