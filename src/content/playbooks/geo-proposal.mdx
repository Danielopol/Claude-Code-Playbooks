## What This Does

Takes the output of a GEO audit and writes the proposal you would otherwise assemble by hand. GEO means Generative Engine Optimization: getting a site cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews rather than ranked in a list of blue links.

Claude reads the audit, picks the three findings that hurt the most, rewrites them as business problems instead of technical ones, selects a service tier from the score, and fills a full proposal document. Executive summary, market context, score breakdown, three packages, ROI projection, engagement timeline, investment summary, next steps, terms.

The proposal ships priced in euros at €2,500 / €5,000 / €9,500 per month. Swap the currency and the numbers for your market before the first send. The structure survives any price point.

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create a Project Folder

```bash
mkdir -p ~/Documents/GEOAgency
```

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above, then:
```bash
mv ~/Downloads/CLAUDE.md ~/Documents/GEOAgency/
```

### Step 3: Bring an Audit

```bash
cd ~/Documents/GEOAgency
claude
```

Paste your audit output, or say "Write a GEO proposal for electron-srl.com." Claude asks for the audit if it cannot find one, because a proposal without findings is a template, not a pitch.

Proposals save to `~/.geo-prospects/proposals/<domain>-proposal-<date>.md`. Pair this with the [GEO Prospect Manager](/playbooks/geo-prospect) and the proposal also flips the prospect's pipeline status.

## How the Tier Gets Picked

Claude recommends one package based on the audit score, then marks it in the packages section and names it in the executive summary.

| GEO Score | Recommended tier | Reasoning |
|-----------|------------------|-----------|
| 0-40 | Premium (€9,500/mo) | Critical issues need intensive work |
| 41-60 | Standard (€5,000/mo) | Significant gaps, structured monthly work |
| 61-75 | Basic (€2,500/mo) | Solid base, monitoring and targeted fixes |
| 76+ | Basic or quarterly retainer | Maintenance only |

Presenting three tiers and letting the client pick shifts the decision onto them. The template presents three and recommends one.

## What Goes Into the Proposal

**Executive summary** — company, industry, score, tier label, the three most urgent issues each with a one-sentence business impact, and the recommended package with its price. Capped at 200 words, because that plus the price is what a decision-maker actually reads.

**Market context** — a table of the numbers that make the case: AI-referred traffic up 527% year over year, AI traffic converting at 4.4x organic, Gartner projecting a 50% drop in traditional search traffic by 2028, and only 23% of marketers investing in GEO today.

**Position table** — the prospect's score, crawler access, brand mentions, schema coverage, and llms.txt status against industry average and top performers.

**Score breakdown** — six weighted categories: AI Citability and Visibility (25%), Brand Authority Signals (20%), Content Quality and E-E-A-T (20%), Technical Foundations (15%), Structured Data (10%), Platform Optimization (10%).

**Critical issues** — for each one: what we found, business impact, our fix, timeline.

**ROI projection** — four scenarios from no action to Premium, with projected score, AI traffic increase, and estimated monthly value, plus stated assumptions and a payback period.

**Timeline** — Month 1 foundation, Months 2-3 optimization, Months 4-6 authority building, Month 6 review, each with an expected score delta.

## Rules It Follows

- **Business language in client-facing text.** "Your product pages have no structured data, so AI assistants cannot read your prices" beats "missing Product schema JSON-LD."
- **Every number traces back to the audit.** No invented traffic figures. If traffic data is missing, the ROI table gets labeled illustrative.
- **No unfilled placeholders.** If a value is unknown, Claude asks or drops the row rather than shipping `[COMPANY NAME]` to a prospect.
- **No ranking guarantees.** The terms guarantee methodology and effort, not outcomes.

## Limitations

- The proposal is only as good as the audit behind it. This playbook writes the document; it does not scan the site. The full audit suite with crawler checks, citability scoring, and schema analysis lives in the [source repo](https://github.com/zubair-trabzada/geo-seo-claude).
- Pricing assumes a European agency selling to SMBs. US, UK, and APAC markets price differently.
- ROI projections use industry benchmarks, not the client's analytics. Treat them as directional and say so in the call.
- The market stats are current as of 2026 and will age. Refresh the numbers table before you lean on it in a pitch.
