## What This Does

GEO is Generative Engine Optimization: getting a site cited by AI search platforms instead of buried under them. Running the analysis is one job. Packaging it into something a business owner will read is another.

This playbook handles the second job. It gives Claude a fixed 12-section report structure, a weighted scoring formula, and the exact client-facing language for translating each technical finding into business impact. Output is a single self-contained `GEO-CLIENT-REPORT.md`, 3,000-6,000 words, with no references to other files.

It comes from the geo-report skill in Zubair Trabzada's geo-seo-claude suite.

## The Scoring Formula

Five components, weighted:

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| AI Platform Readiness | 25% |
| Content Quality & E-E-A-T | 25% |
| Technical Foundation | 20% |
| Schema & Structured Data | 15% |
| Brand Authority & Entity Presence | 15% |

The composite lands in one of five bands, each with client-facing copy attached so the number arrives with an interpretation:

| Range | Label |
|---|---|
| 85-100 | Excellent |
| 70-84 | Good |
| 55-69 | Moderate |
| 40-54 | Below Average |
| 0-39 | Needs Attention |

## What Goes In the Report

Twelve sections, in order:

1. **Executive summary** — one paragraph, 4-6 sentences, score plus the top three recommendations plus a dollar estimate
2. **GEO readiness score** — headline number and weighted component breakdown
3. **AI visibility dashboard** — per-platform readiness for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Web Search, Perplexity, Gemini, and Bing Copilot
4. **AI crawler access** — allow/block status for Googlebot, GPTBot, Bingbot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, ClaudeBot, Applebot-Extended
5. **Brand authority** — presence on Wikipedia, Wikidata, LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit, Knowledge Panel, Crunchbase, GitHub
6. **Citability analysis** — the five most and five least citable pages, each with a specific fix
7. **Technical health** — Core Web Vitals, SSR, mobile, security, speed, IndexNow
8. **Schema and structured data** — which JSON-LD types exist and validate
9. **llms.txt status**
10. **Prioritized action plan** — the section clients actually act on
11. **Competitor comparison** — included only when competitor URLs were analyzed
12. **Appendix** — methodology, data sources, and a 14-term glossary

## The Action Plan Tiers

The plan sorts every recommendation into three buckets by effort, so nobody has to guess what to do Monday morning.

**Quick wins, under 4 hours.** Unblock AI crawlers in robots.txt. Add publication dates. Add author bylines with credentials. Fix broken meta descriptions. Add `sameAs` properties to existing Organization schema. Create llms.txt.

**Medium-term, 1-5 days.** Restructure the top 10 pages with question-based headings and direct answers. Implement full Schema.org markup. Build author pages with credentials. Optimize Core Web Vitals. Register Bing Webmaster Tools. Implement IndexNow.

**Strategic, weeks to months.** Build Wikipedia and Wikidata entity presence. Develop Reddit community engagement. Launch a YouTube content strategy. Move to server-side rendering. Build topical authority. Start an original research program.

## Client-Facing Translations

The template carries prewritten explanations so technical findings land with a non-technical reader. Two examples it uses verbatim:

> On blocked crawlers: "Blocking AI crawlers is like closing your store during business hours. If a crawler cannot access your site, the AI platform it powers cannot cite your content."

> On client-side rendering: "Your site uses client-side rendering, which means AI crawlers see an empty page when they visit. This is the single most impactful technical issue for AI search visibility."

The brand authority table carries citation-share stats for weight, including that 47.9% of ChatGPT citations point to Wikipedia and 46.7% of Perplexity citations point to Reddit.

## Tips

- Fill the five input areas before you ask for the report. Platform readiness, content and E-E-A-T, technical, schema, and brand authority each feed a weighted slot.
- Missing data gets marked "Not assessed" rather than estimated. Keep it that way. A fabricated Core Web Vitals number is the fastest way to lose a client's trust.
- Dollar figures anchor on three assumptions the template states openly: current organic traffic value, AI search reaching 25-40% of organic discovery by end of 2026, and a 10-point score gain correlating with a 15-25% lift in citation frequency. Adjust them for your market.
- Pair this with the PDF renderer when the deliverable needs to look like a consulting document rather than a text file.

## Limitations

- The report is only as good as the inputs. This playbook formats and frames findings, it does not crawl or measure a site on its own.
- The composite score is a weighted heuristic from the geo-seo-claude suite, not an industry standard. It tracks progress against itself well, and comparisons across vendors poorly.
- Impact estimates come from 2025-2026 industry benchmarks. Treat them as ranges, state assumptions, and never guarantee results.
- Section 11 needs competitor URLs analyzed alongside the primary domain, otherwise skip it.
