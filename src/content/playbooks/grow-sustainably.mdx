## What This Does

A decision-review skill for any spend, hire, or fundraise question. It evaluates choices against:

1. Impact on profitability (revenue and cost implications)
2. Reversibility (avoid long leases, big hires, bad VC terms)
3. Whether the driver is customer need or ego/vanity
4. Whether a cheaper/simpler alternative exists
5. The "default alive or default dead" test

Covers cost-cutting rules (pay yourself little, hire software not humans, no office, no SF, outsource first), fundraising alternatives (Earnest Capital, Indie.vc, Tinyseed, Regulation Crowdfunding), and co-founder relationship hygiene.

## The Two Categories of Fatal Mistakes

1. **Running out of money** — solved by profitability
2. **Running out of energy** — equally fatal, more often ignored

## Cost-Cutting Rules That Actually Work

- Pay yourself as little as possible at the start (Sahil paid himself $0 in 2015)
- Hire software before humans — Pilot/Bench, Gusto, Zapier
- No office, no SF — remote compounds into profitability
- Outsource to freelancers before hiring FTEs

## Pre-Cofounder Conversation Checklist

Treat it like a marriage:
- What does a happy relationship look like?
- What does success look like?
- What does an exit look like?
- How fast do we want to grow?
- Why are we starting this together?

Use vesting. Have hard conversations early — they only get harder.

## Who Should Use This

Bootstrapped founders who need a cold check on spending, operators weighing VC vs. profitability, small-business owners reading blitzscaling advice and wondering if it applies to them. It doesn't.
