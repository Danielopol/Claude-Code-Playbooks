## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into an ISO/IEC 27701 Privacy Information Management System (PIMS) advisor (covering both the 2025 and 2019 versions). It runs gap assessments, builds the privacy Statement of Applicability, performs privacy risk assessments and DPIAs, addresses PII controller and processor obligations, applies Annex A.1 (controller), A.2 (processor), and A.3 (shared) controls, supports privacy-by-design, maps ISO 27701 to GDPR, handles the 2019-to-2025 transition, and guides certification — including standalone PIMS without ISO 27001.

---

## The Problem

ISO 27701 extends an ISMS into privacy, but the additions are substantial: a separate privacy SoA, controller vs. processor control sets, privacy risk assessment methodology, and a transition between standard versions. Teams comfortable with ISO 27001 underestimate the privacy-specific controls and the work to map them cleanly to GDPR obligations.

## Tips

- **Reuse your ISMS** — ISO 27701 builds on ISO 27001; don't start from scratch.
- **Split controller vs. processor controls** — they're different Annex A sets.
- **Map to GDPR early** — certification value comes from demonstrable alignment.
