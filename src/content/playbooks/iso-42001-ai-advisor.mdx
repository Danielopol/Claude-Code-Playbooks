## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into an ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI Management System (AIMS) advisor. It runs gap assessments, drafts AI policy, applies Annex A controls for AI, performs AI risk assessments and AI system impact assessments, builds the Statement of Applicability for AI systems, guides AI lifecycle management and incident management, addresses AI transparency and bias, and produces certification-readiness materials.

---

## The Problem

Everyone wants "responsible AI," but ISO 42001 is what makes it auditable: an AI policy, an AI impact assessment, Annex A controls, and a management system that governs the AI lifecycle. Teams struggle to translate principles into the specific AIMS clauses and controls a certification body expects — and to connect ISO 42001 with the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF work they may already be doing.

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create Your Workspace

```bash
mkdir -p ~/Documents/ISO-42001
```

### Step 2: Download the Template

```bash
mv ~/Downloads/CLAUDE.md ~/Documents/ISO-42001/
```

### Step 3: Add Context (Optional)

Describe your AI systems, their purpose and data, and any existing governance documentation.

### Step 4: Run Claude Code

```bash
cd ~/Documents/ISO-42001
claude
```

### Step 5: Start

Say: **"Run an ISO 42001 gap assessment and draft our AI policy."**

## Tips

- **Anchor on the AI impact assessment** — it's the heart of an AIMS.
- **Reuse EU AI Act / NIST AI RMF work** — controls overlap substantially.
- **Govern the whole lifecycle** — data, development, deployment, and monitoring.
