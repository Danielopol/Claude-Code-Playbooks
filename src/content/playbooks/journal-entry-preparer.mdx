## What This Does

This playbook helps you prepare properly formatted journal entries with debits, credits, supporting calculations, and audit-ready documentation. It supports common entry types -- AP accruals, fixed asset depreciation, prepaid amortization, payroll accruals, and revenue recognition -- and walks through gathering source data, calculating amounts, and generating entries with a review checklist.

**Important**: This workflow assists with journal entry preparation but does not provide financial advice. All entries should be reviewed by qualified financial professionals before posting.

## Supported Entry Types

- `ap-accrual` -- Accounts payable accruals for goods/services received but not yet invoiced
- `fixed-assets` -- Depreciation and amortization entries for fixed assets and intangibles
- `prepaid` -- Amortization of prepaid expenses (insurance, software, rent, etc.)
- `payroll` -- Payroll accruals including salaries, benefits, taxes, and bonus accruals
- `revenue` -- Revenue recognition entries including deferred revenue adjustments

---

## Review Checklist

Every generated entry includes a built-in review checklist covering:

- Debits equal credits
- Correct accounting period and account codes
- Memo clarity for audit purposes
- Department/cost center coding
- Reversal flag for accruals
- Consistency with prior period treatment
- Supporting documentation references
