## What This Does

Plans lead magnets that capture emails, generate qualified leads, and lead naturally to product adoption — covering ebooks, checklists, templates, swipe files, mini-courses, and Notion/spreadsheet templates.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create a Project Folder

Create a dedicated folder for this workflow (e.g. `~/marketing/lead-magnets`).

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above and save the file as `CLAUDE.md` in that folder.

### Step 3: Run the Workflow

Describe your product, audience, and existing lead capture motion.

---

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Business context** — product, ICP, problems
- **Current lead generation** — what's working, what isn't
- **Resources** — design help, time available
- **Distribution channels** — blog, social, paid, partners

## How It Works

1. Identify the sharp, narrow problem your ICP wants solved right now
2. Match it to the strongest format (checklist, template, swipe file, mini-course, ebook)
3. Outline the magnet — bare minimum content that delivers the promise
4. Plan the opt-in (page copy, fields, friction)
5. Plan the post-capture sequence (welcome, value, soft pitch)
6. Plan distribution (blog upgrade, social, paid, partners)

## What You Get

- 2–3 lead magnet concepts with rationale
- Outline for the chosen magnet
- Opt-in page copy
- Post-capture email sequence outline
- Distribution plan
