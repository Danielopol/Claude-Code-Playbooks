## What This Does

Walks through 8 canvas boxes in sequence: **Business Problem → Business Outcomes → Users → User Outcomes & Benefits → Solutions → Hypotheses → Learn First → Experiments**. Each box has quality checks, examples, and challenge prompts for common failure modes.

Built on Jeff Gothelf's Lean UX Canvas v2. Pairs with `problem-statement`, `proto-persona`, `jobs-to-be-done`, `epic-hypothesis`, and `pol-probe-advisor` for experiment selection.

## The 8 Boxes

| # | Box | Core Question |
|---|-----|---------------|
| 1 | Business Problem | What changed in the world? |
| 2 | Business Outcomes | What measurable behavior change = success? |
| 3 | Users | Which persona first? |
| 4 | User Outcomes & Benefits | Why would users seek this? |
| 5 | Solutions | What might work? (3+ options, not commitments) |
| 6 | Hypotheses | "We believe [outcome] if [user] attains [benefit] with [solution]" |
| 7 | Learn First | What's the riskiest assumption right now? |
| 8 | Experiments | Smallest test to validate/invalidate? |

---

## Key Distinction: Box 2 vs. Box 4

- **Box 2 (Business Outcomes)** — Metrics, behavior change: "Mobile conversion 45% → 60%"
- **Box 4 (User Outcomes & Benefits)** — Emotions, empathy: "Avoid embarrassment of failed checkout"

Confusing these is the single most common canvas failure.

---

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Force 3+ solutions in Box 5.** One solution = stakeholders already decided.
- **Early on, prioritize value risk over feasibility.** Don't build a feasible thing users don't want.
- **If your experiment takes >2 weeks, break it down.** Canvas is about learning speed.
- **Re-visit the canvas after each experiment.** Update hypotheses; pick next riskiest assumption.
- **Prioritize the riskiest assumption, not the easiest to test.**

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Starting with solutions, not problems (Box 1 says "build X")
- Vague outcomes ("increase revenue" with no behavior change metric)
- Too-broad users ("all users" prevents targeted experiments)
- Confusing Box 2 (metrics) with Box 4 (empathy)
- Listing only one solution in Box 5
- Skipping experiments — "we'll build it and see"
