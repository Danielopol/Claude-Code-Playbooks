## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into an LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, Law 13,709/2018) compliance advisor for Brazil. It runs gap assessments, maps legal bases for processing, handles sensitive data and data-subject rights, plans DPO (encarregado) appointment and ANPD breach notification, drafts privacy policies and DPIAs for Brazilian operations, designs consent management, and compares LGPD with GDPR.

---

## The Problem

LGPD looks like GDPR but has its own legal bases, its own regulator (ANPD), and its own enforcement and penalty regime (fines up to 2% of Brazilian revenue, capped at R$50M per violation). Companies assume GDPR compliance carries over and miss Brazil-specific requirements: ANPD breach notification, local DPO expectations, distinct legal bases, and international-transfer rules out of Brazil.

## Tips

- **Don't assume GDPR coverage** — map LGPD legal bases explicitly; they differ.
- **Plan ANPD notification** before an incident forces it.
- **Localize the policy** — Portuguese-language, Brazil-specific disclosures matter.

---

## Important Disclaimer

This is a compliance support tool, not legal advice. LGPD interpretation evolves with ANPD rulemaking. Have qualified Brazilian privacy counsel review outputs before relying on them.
