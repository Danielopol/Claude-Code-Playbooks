## What This Does

Surfaces the right marketing ideas for your specific product, stage, and resources from a library of 139 proven SaaS marketing tactics — and explains how to execute the ones you pick.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create a Project Folder

Create a dedicated folder for this workflow (e.g. `~/marketing/marketing-ideas`).

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above and save the file as `CLAUDE.md` in that folder.

### Step 3: Run the Workflow

Tell Claude about your product, audience, stage, and constraints.

---

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Product and audience**
- **Stage** — pre-launch, launched, scaling
- **Resources** — time, budget, team size
- **What you've already tried**
- **Goals** — leads, signups, awareness, revenue

## How It Works

1. Filter the idea library by stage, audience type, and effort
2. Surface 3–5 ideas most relevant to the situation
3. Explain why each fits and what success looks like
4. Detail execution steps for the chosen ideas
5. Sequence them (start with quick wins, then bigger bets)

## What You Get

- 3–5 tailored marketing ideas with rationale
- Execution plan per idea (steps, owner, timeline)
- Resource estimate and expected outcomes
- Sequencing recommendation
