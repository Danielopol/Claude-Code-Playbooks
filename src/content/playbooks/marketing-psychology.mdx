## What This Does

Helps apply psychological principles and mental models — anchoring, social proof, loss aversion, framing, scarcity, reciprocity, and more — to marketing decisions and creative work, ethically.

Describe the marketing problem, page, or campaign.

## Inputs You'll Need

- **The marketing problem or asset** — page, ad, sequence, decision
- **Audience** — who you're trying to influence
- **Goal** — conversion, retention, perception change
- **Constraints** — brand tone, ethical guardrails, regulatory limits

## How It Works

1. Identify which mental models apply to the situation
2. Explain the underlying psychology in plain language
3. Translate to specific marketing applications (copy, layout, sequence, offer)
4. Recommend ethical guardrails — persuasion, not manipulation
5. Suggest tests to validate impact

## What You Get

- Shortlist of relevant mental models with brief explanations
- Specific applications for your asset or campaign
- Copy or design recommendations grounded in each principle
- Ethical guardrails and what to avoid
- Test ideas to measure the impact
