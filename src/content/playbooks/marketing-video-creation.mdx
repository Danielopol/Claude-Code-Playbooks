## What This Does

Helps produce marketing video content using AI generation models (Veo, Runway, Kling, Pika), AI avatars (HeyGen, Synthesia), and programmatic frameworks (Remotion, Hyperframes) — for product demos, explainers, testimonials, social clips, and ads.

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Video goal** — product demo, explainer, testimonial, social clip, ad, tutorial
- **Platform** — YouTube, TikTok/Reels/Shorts, website, ad, sales deck
- **Length** — 15s, 30s, 60s, 2 min, 5 min
- **Presenter** — AI avatar, voiceover, screen recording, B-roll
- **Brand assets** — logo, colors, fonts, footage

## How It Works

1. Pick the right tool stack for the goal and length
2. Write the script with a strong hook and clear value beat
3. Plan the shot list / scene structure
4. Generate or capture each scene
5. Apply brand assets (logo, colors, lower-thirds)
6. Export at platform-specific specs (aspect, length, captions)

## What You Get

- Tool recommendation and reasoning
- Script with hook, value beats, and CTA
- Shot list / scene structure
- Generation prompts or recording guide
- Final exports for each target platform
