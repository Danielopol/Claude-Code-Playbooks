## What This Does

Builds a marketing plan that starts with time, not money. Output includes:

1. Your primary content platform and cadence
2. 5 content ideas each across Educate, Inspire, and Entertain
3. An email-list strategy (what to offer, how to collect)
4. A build-in-public plan
5. A clear trigger for when (if ever) to turn on paid advertising

## Prerequisites

Don't start here unless you have:
- A community you belong to
- A product people are paying for
- ~100 customers (repeat customers = PMF)
- Experience selling one-on-one

Sales informs marketing. If you haven't sold manually, skip to `first-100-customers` first.

## The Customer Journey (5 Steps, No Skipping)

1. Engage → encounter your content
2. Follow → find you interesting enough to follow
3. Research → check out your product
4. Consider → evaluate pricing, features
5. Buy → convert

## Three Levels of Content

- **Level 1: Educate** — share what you've learned (100 customers = 100 things to share)
- **Level 2: Inspire** — share your journey, struggles, wins
- **Level 3: Entertain** — the hardest, widest-reaching

## Own Your Audience

Social media = rented land. Email = owned land. Start the list immediately.

## When to Spend Money

Only after organic traction tells you exactly who your customer is. Then use lookalike audiences from existing customers. Never pay more than you make per customer.

## Who Should Use This

Founders who've hit ~100 customers and are ready to scale without torching cash on ads. Solo operators who need a sustainable content cadence. Anyone who's been told to "do marketing" and doesn't know where to start.
