## What This Does

Covers the full lifecycle of an empirical ML paper as one iterative loop, not a strict sequence: project setup and contribution framing, a breadth-then-depth literature search with a mandatory citation-verification protocol (never generate a bibliography entry from memory), experiment design where every experiment must map to a specific claim, crash-safe execution with an experiment journal that captures the reasoning tree git alone can't, and result analysis that treats a well-explained negative result as genuinely publishable rather than automatically a dead end.

The two most load-bearing pieces are the **experiment log** — a structured bridge from raw result files to prose that prevents the drafting pass from hallucinating numbers it had to re-derive — and the **self-review phase**, which runs 3-5 independent reviewer personas biased toward negative findings, aggregates them with a meta-reviewer, adds a separate visual pass on the compiled PDF and a fresh-context claim-verification pass, before ever reaching submission prep (anonymization, formatting, and automated pre-compilation checks that catch broken citations and missing figures before the LaTeX compiler does).

## Tips & Best Practices

- Revisit the one-sentence contribution from Phase 0 every time a result changes the story — a paper that quietly drifts from its stated contribution reads as unfocused even when every individual section is solid.
- Treat "did they compare against X" as the default reviewer question and make sure the baseline table already answers it before submission — weak baselines are one of the most common rejection reasons.
- Don't force a positive spin a negative result doesn't support. Several strong venues (workshops, TMLR, NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks) explicitly want honestly reported null results with rigorous methodology.

## Limitations

- Built around empirical ML papers for major ML/AI venues — a theory paper, a survey, or a different field's conventions need real adaptation, not a literal pass through every phase.
- Citation verification and human-evaluation design assume access to real APIs and platforms (a citation database, a crowdsourcing service) — without them, budget more manual verification time.
- A process framework, not a guarantee of acceptance — rigorous baselines and honest limitations sections improve the odds, but reviewer judgment is still reviewer judgment.
