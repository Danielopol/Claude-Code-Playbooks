## What This Does

Acts as a world-class panel moderator prep tool. Provide the event details, panelists, and goals, and it generates a complete moderator guide with opening script, 12 sharp questions with follow-ups, a lightning round, and audience prompts.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template

Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Define the Panel

Fill in event name, session title, audience, panelists, goals, and duration.

### Step 3: Generate the Guide

```bash
claude
```

Say: **"Create a moderator guide for my panel"**

---

## What You Get

| Output | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Opening Script** | 30-second intro |
| **Agenda** | Minute-by-minute breakdown |
| **12 Questions** | Each with follow-up + trapdoor if they dodge |
| **Lightning Round** | 6 rapid-fire questions |
| **Audience Prompts** | 5 audience engagement starters |
| **Closing Script** | 20-second wrap-up |

---

## Example Output

```markdown
## Opening (30 seconds)
Welcome to "The Future of Decentralized Identity."
We have 45 minutes and three builders who've shipped
identity products to over 1M users combined...

## Questions
- Q1: Your product handles 500K daily verifications.
  What breaks first at 5M?
  - Follow up: How do you handle that without
    centralizing?
  - Trapdoor if they dodge: Your competitors say
    you already centralized. What do they see that
    users don't?

## Lightning Round
- What's one thing your industry gets wrong?
- Name a competitor you respect and why.
- What's the most expensive mistake you've made?
```

---

## Tips

- **Name panelists**: Include their background for personalized questions
- **Set goals**: "Extract real product metrics" vs "keep it entertaining"
- **Specify topics to avoid**: Prevents awkward moments
- **Use trapdoors**: The "trapdoor" follow-ups push past rehearsed answers
