## What This Does

Converts free users to paid (or upgrades users to higher tiers) at moments when they've experienced enough value to justify the commitment — covering paywalls, upgrade screens, upsell modals, feature gates, trial expiration screens, and limit-reached prompts.

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Upgrade context** — freemium → paid, trial → paid, tier upgrade, feature unlock, usage limit
- **Product model** — what's free, what's gated, what triggers prompts
- **User journey** — what they were trying to do when the prompt appeared
- **Current conversion rate** if known

## How It Works

1. Map the upgrade moment to the value the user just experienced
2. Anchor copy to that experience, not generic features
3. Show the right plan comparison treatment (one-click default vs side-by-side)
4. Add social proof and risk reversal where they fit
5. Remove unnecessary friction (pricing surprises, extra fields, dead-ends)
6. Recommend tests with hypotheses

## What You Get

- Annotated paywall recommendations with copy and layout
- Plan comparison treatment
- Friction audit (what to remove)
- Social proof and risk reversal placements
- A/B test backlog
