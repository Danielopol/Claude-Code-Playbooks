## What This Does

Generates comprehensive due diligence checklists tailored to the target company's sector, deal type (platform, add-on, growth equity, recap, carve-out), and complexity level. Covers all major workstreams with request lists, status tracking, and red flag escalation.

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create a Project Folder

Create a folder for your deal's diligence tracking and place the downloaded template inside as `CLAUDE.md`.

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above, then move the file into your project folder as `CLAUDE.md`.

### Step 3: Start Working

```
"Generate a DD checklist for a SaaS company acquisition"
"Create a diligence request list for a carve-out deal"
"What do we still need from the data room?"
```

## Workstreams Covered

| Workstream | Key Items |
|-----------|-----------|
| Financial | QoE, working capital, debt-like items, tax, capex |
| Commercial | TAM/SAM, customer analysis, pricing, pipeline |
| Legal | Corporate structure, contracts, litigation, IP, compliance |
| Operational | Management, org structure, IT, supply chain, facilities |
| HR / People | Headcount, compensation, benefits, retention risk |
| IT / Technology | Tech stack, technical debt, cybersecurity, data privacy |
| Environmental / ESG | Liabilities, compliance history, ESG risks |

## Sector-Specific Additions

The checklist automatically adds relevant items based on sector:

- **Software/SaaS**: ARR quality, cohort analysis, hosting costs, SOC2
- **Healthcare**: Regulatory approvals, reimbursement risk, payor mix
- **Industrial**: Equipment condition, environmental remediation, safety record
- **Financial services**: Regulatory capital, compliance history, credit quality
- **Consumer**: Brand health, channel mix, seasonality, inventory management

## Tips & Best Practices

- Prioritize P0 items that are gating to LOI or close
- Flag items where the seller is slow to respond — delays may indicate issues
- Cross-reference data room contents against the checklist to identify gaps
- Update the checklist as diligence progresses — it is a living document
- Status tracking uses: Not Started, Requested, Received, In Review, Complete, Red Flag
