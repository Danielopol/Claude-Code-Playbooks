## What This Does

Runs a 3-step deal sourcing pipeline: discover target companies matching your investment criteria, check for existing relationships via email and internal communications, and draft personalized cold outreach emails to founders and CEOs.


## The 3-Step Pipeline

1. **Discover**: Research and shortlist companies based on sector, revenue range, geography, ownership type, and growth profile
2. **CRM Check**: Search email and internal channels for prior correspondence — flag each company as New, Existing, or Previously Passed
3. **Outreach**: Draft personalized cold emails to founders/CEOs with specific references to their business

## Outreach Email Guidelines

- **Length**: 4-6 sentences max — founders are busy
- **Tone**: Professional but warm, not overly formal
- **Structure**: Brief intro, why this company caught your attention, what you are looking for, soft ask
- **Personalization**: Reference the company's specific product, recent news, or market position — never use generic templates
- Subject line: Keep it short and specific, not "Investment Opportunity"

## Tips & Best Practices

- Always present the shortlist for user review before drafting emails
- Never send emails without explicit user approval
- Prioritize quality over quantity — 5 well-researched targets beat 20 generic ones
- No attachments on first touch
- If the user's firm intro or investment criteria are not clear, ask before drafting
