Structures a complete post-acquisition value creation plan with revenue growth levers, margin expansion opportunities, and strategic initiatives mapped to an EBITDA bridge over the hold period. Includes a 100-day plan, KPI dashboard, and accountability framework.

## Value Creation Levers

**Revenue Growth:**
- Organic growth (price increases, volume, market expansion)
- Cross-sell / upsell to existing customers
- New market entry (geography, verticals, channels)
- Sales force effectiveness
- Add-on M&A

**Margin Expansion:**
- Pricing optimization
- COGS reduction and procurement savings
- OpEx optimization and shared services
- Technology and automation investment
- Scale leverage

**Strategic / Multiple Expansion:**
- Platform building via add-ons
- Recurring revenue shift
- Management upgrades
- ESG and governance improvements

## The 100-Day Plan

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| Stabilize & Assess | 1-30 | Management alignment, quick wins, operational assessment, reporting setup |
| Plan & Initiate | 31-60 | Strategic plan communication, top 5 initiatives launch, key hires, M&A pipeline |
| Execute & Measure | 61-100 | First results from quick wins, first board meeting, progress report, plan adjustment |

## Tips & Best Practices

- Be realistic about timing — most PE value creation takes 12-24 months to show in financials
- Quick wins matter for momentum and credibility, but do not over-rotate on cost cuts at the expense of growth
- Management buy-in is critical — co-develop the plan, do not impose it
- Track initiative-level P&L impact, not just top-line EBITDA — you need to know what is working
- Add-on M&A is often the largest value creation lever — start the pipeline on Day 1
- Always pressure-test assumptions with operating partners or industry experts
