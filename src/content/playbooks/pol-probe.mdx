## What This Does

Template for documenting a Proof of Life probe with 5 essential characteristics (lightweight, disposable, narrow scope, brutally honest, tiny & focused), explicit disposal plan, and falsifiable success/fail criteria.

Pairs with `pol-probe-advisor` (decision tree for which probe type) and `discovery-process` (where probes live in the cycle).

---

## Quick Start

```bash
mkdir -p ~/Documents/PolProbe
mv ~/Downloads/CLAUDE.md ~/Documents/PolProbe/
cd ~/Documents/PolProbe
claude
```

Provide hypothesis, risk, timeline, and chosen probe type. Claude produces the fully filled template with success criteria, tools, and disposal plan.

## The 5 Essential Characteristics

| Characteristic | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Lightweight** | Hours/days, not weeks |
| **Disposable** | Planned for deletion, not scaling |
| **Narrow Scope** | One hypothesis, one risk |
| **Brutally Honest** | Harsh truths, not vanity metrics |
| **Tiny & Focused** | Reconnaissance, never MVP |

If your probe feels too polished to delete, it's prototype theater.

---

## PoL Probe vs. MVP

- **PoL Probe:** Hours-days, disposable, tests IF you should build
- **MVP:** Weeks-months, iterated, ships what you build

Probes are pre-MVP reconnaissance.

---

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Set disposal date before building.** Without one, the probe becomes a product.
- **Write success/fail/learn criteria before building.** "We'll know it when we see it" = no truth.
- **One probe, one hypothesis.** Bundle 3 questions = ambiguous results.
- **Choose method AFTER hypothesis.** Tools-first biases toward what you can build, not what you need to learn.
- **Celebrate the jank.** Frankensoft is the point — a clean probe defeats its purpose.

---

## Common Pitfalls

- Running broad "will users like this?" tests (not falsifiable)
- Treating the probe like a proto-MVP and refusing to delete it
- Vanity metrics that avoid uncomfortable truth
- No pre-defined failure threshold before testing starts
- Choosing tools before defining the hypothesis
