## What This Does

Designs popups, modals, overlays, slide-ins, and sticky banners that convert without trashing brand perception — covering email capture, lead-magnet delivery, discounts, announcements, exit-intent saves, and feature promotion.

---

### Step 3: Run the Workflow

Describe the popup purpose, audience, and the page or moment where it appears.

---

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Popup purpose** — email capture, lead magnet, discount, announcement, exit save, feature promo
- **Trigger** — time on page, scroll depth, exit intent, click, sticky bar
- **Audience and offer**
- **Frequency rules** — how often each user sees it
- **Brand and design constraints**

## How It Works

1. Match purpose to the right popup pattern
2. Write a sharp, value-first headline and subheading
3. Choose form length (email-only vs more fields)
4. Set targeting and frequency rules so users aren't ambushed repeatedly
5. Plan mobile treatment (popups behave differently on mobile)
6. Define A/B tests for trigger, offer, copy, and design

## What You Get

- Recommended popup pattern with full copy
- Trigger and frequency rules
- Mobile-specific treatment
- Design notes (sizing, animation, dismissibility)
- A/B test plan
