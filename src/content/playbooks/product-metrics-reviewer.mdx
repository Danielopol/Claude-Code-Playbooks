## What This Does

Reviews and analyzes product metrics using a structured hierarchy — North Star metric, L1 health indicators (acquisition, activation, engagement, retention, revenue, satisfaction), and L2 diagnostic metrics. Produces scorecards, trend analysis, bright spots, areas of concern, and recommended actions.

## Metrics Hierarchy

| Level | What It Covers |
|-------|---------------|
| **North Star** | Single metric capturing core value delivered to users |
| **L1 Health** | Acquisition, activation, engagement, retention, monetization, satisfaction |
| **L2 Diagnostic** | Funnel conversions, feature adoption, segment breakdowns, performance |

---

## Review Cadences

- **Weekly** (15-30 min) — North Star, key L1 movements, experiment results, anomalies
- **Monthly** (30-60 min) — Full L1 scorecard, OKR progress, cohort analysis, feature adoption
- **Quarterly** (60-90 min) — OKR scoring, trend analysis, YoY comparisons, retrospective

---

## Tips

- Always show comparisons — absolute numbers without context are useless
- Segment analysis often reveals that an aggregate metric masks important differences
- If a metric misses its target, recommend what to do about it, not just report the miss
- Focus attention on meaningful changes — small fluctuations are noise
