## What This Does

Compares your organization's key metrics against industry benchmarks, identifies gaps and strengths, and produces improvement recommendations with prioritized action plans.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template

Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Gather Your Metrics

Prepare your KPIs, operational metrics, and any industry benchmark data you have.

### Step 3: Start Using It

```bash
claude
```

Say: **"Benchmark our SaaS metrics against industry standards. Here's our data: ARR $8M, net retention 105%, CAC payback 18 months, gross margin 72%."**

---

## Analysis Output

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Benchmark Summary** | Your metrics vs. industry medians/top quartile |
| **Strengths** | Where you outperform benchmarks |
| **Gap Analysis** | Where you're below standard with severity |
| **Best Practices** | What top performers do differently |
| **Improvement Roadmap** | Prioritized actions to close gaps |
| **Target Setting** | Realistic improvement targets with timelines |

---

## Tips

- **Use the right peer group**: Series B SaaS ≠ public company benchmarks
- **Context matters**: Below-benchmark churn might be fine if you're in a niche market
- **Focus on trends, not snapshots**: Are you improving or declining vs. benchmarks?
- **Benchmark processes, not just outcomes**: HOW top performers achieve results matters

---

## Troubleshooting

**Benchmarks feel irrelevant**
Specify: "Use Series A B2B SaaS benchmarks, not overall SaaS"

**Too many gaps identified**
Ask: "Prioritize the 3 gaps with the biggest revenue impact"

**No action from benchmarking**
End with: "Each gap needs a specific owner, target, and quarterly checkpoint"
