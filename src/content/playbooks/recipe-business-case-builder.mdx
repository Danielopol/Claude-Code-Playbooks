## What This Does

Creates structured business cases for investment decisions — product launches, tool purchases, hiring requests, strategic initiatives — with financial projections, ROI calculations, risk analysis, and stakeholder-ready recommendations.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template

Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Gather Your Case Data

Collect: cost estimates, expected benefits, timelines, risks, and alternatives considered.

### Step 3: Start Using It

```bash
claude
```

Say: **"Build a business case for hiring 3 engineers. Investment: $600K/year. Expected impact: ship enterprise product 4 months faster."**

---

## Business Case Sections

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Problem Statement** | What problem this solves and cost of inaction |
| **Proposed Solution** | What you're recommending and why |
| **Financial Analysis** | Costs, benefits, ROI, payback period |
| **Alternatives** | Other options considered with trade-offs |
| **Risk Assessment** | What could go wrong, mitigation plans |
| **Implementation Plan** | Timeline, milestones, resources needed |
| **Recommendation** | Clear ask with supporting rationale |

---

## Tips

- **Lead with the cost of NOT acting**: "Every month we delay costs $200K in lost revenue"
- **Show your math**: Assumptions behind projections must be transparent
- **Address the strongest objection proactively**: Don't wait for pushback
- **Include 3 scenarios**: Conservative, base case, and optimistic projections

## Troubleshooting

**Case feels one-sided**
Ask: "Steelman the argument against this investment — then address each point"

**ROI projections seem aggressive**
Specify: "Use conservative assumptions — what's the worst-case ROI?"

**Too long for executives**
Say: "1-page executive summary with detailed appendix — decision on page 1"
