## What This Does

Creates structured diversity and inclusion reports that track representation metrics, analyze hiring and promotion pipeline equity, and measure progress against organizational DEI goals with actionable recommendations.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template
Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Gather Workforce Data
Compile demographic data, hiring stats, promotion rates, and engagement survey results by group.

### Step 3: Start Using It
```bash
claude
```
Say: **"Generate our quarterly DEI report. Analyze representation by level, hiring pipeline conversion rates by demographic, and progress against our 2026 goals."**

## Report Sections

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Representation Dashboard** | Demographics by level, department, function |
| **Pipeline Analysis** | Application → hire conversion by demographic |
| **Promotion Equity** | Promotion rates across demographic groups |
| **Retention Parity** | Attrition differences by group |
| **Goal Tracking** | Progress against stated DEI commitments |
| **Recommendations** | Data-driven actions to close gaps |

---

## Tips
- **Intersectionality matters**: Analyze combinations (e.g., women in engineering), not just single dimensions
- **Pipeline vs. representation**: Low representation often traces to pipeline narrowing at specific stages
- **Statistical significance**: Flag when sample sizes are too small for meaningful comparison
- **Benchmark externally**: Compare against industry averages, not just internal history

## Troubleshooting

**Small sample sizes**
Say: "Flag groups with fewer than 10 people — aggregate or note insufficient data."

**Data privacy concerns**
Specify: "Report at aggregate level only. No demographic data for groups smaller than 5."

**Goals not set yet**
Ask: "Benchmark against industry averages and recommend realistic targets."
