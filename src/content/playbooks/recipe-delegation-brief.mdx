## What This Does

Creates comprehensive delegation briefs that eliminate ambiguity about ownership, decision authority, success criteria, and support structure. Prevents the #1 delegation failure: unclear expectations.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template

Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Define the Task

Know: what's being delegated, to whom, the timeline, and how much authority they have.

### Step 3: Start Using It

```bash
claude
```

Say: **"Create a delegation brief for Sarah to lead the vendor evaluation project. She has 3 weeks and budget authority up to $50K."**

---

## Brief Components

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Assignment Overview** | What, why, and context |
| **Success Criteria** | Clear definition of done |
| **Scope & Boundaries** | What's in/out of scope |
| **Authority Level** | What they can decide independently |
| **Timeline** | Milestones and check-in dates |
| **Support Structure** | Who to escalate to, resources available |
| **Potential Pitfalls** | Common failure modes to watch for |

---

## Tips

- **Walk through it together**: The brief starts conversations, not replaces them
- **Match detail to experience**: Less detail for veterans, more for newer people
- **Make authority explicit**: "You can approve vendors under $50K without checking with me"
- **Schedule early check-ins**: Catch misalignment in week 1, not at deadline

## Troubleshooting

**Brief is too controlling**
Say: "Focus on outcomes and boundaries, not step-by-step instructions"

**Delegate seems confused**
Review authority section: "Make decision rights crystal clear"

**Task scope is creeping**
Update: "Add explicit out-of-scope items to prevent scope creep"
