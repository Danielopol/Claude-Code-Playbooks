## What This Does

Creates structured onboarding programs with day-by-day checklists, role-specific learning paths, stakeholder meeting schedules, and 30-60-90 day milestone plans. Ensures every new hire has a consistent, comprehensive start.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template
Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Define the Role and Team
Specify the role, department, key tools/systems, and stakeholders the new hire needs to meet.

### Step 3: Start Using It
```bash
claude
```
Say: **"Create a 90-day onboarding plan for a new Marketing Manager. Include week 1 day-by-day schedule, key stakeholder meetings, and 30-60-90 day milestones."**

---

## Onboarding Plan Structure

| Phase | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Pre-Start** | Equipment setup, access requests, welcome email |
| **Week 1** | Day-by-day schedule with orientation activities |
| **Weeks 2-4** | Role-specific training and shadowing |
| **Days 30-60** | Independent work with check-ins |
| **Days 60-90** | Full ownership and performance baseline |
| **Stakeholder Map** | Who to meet, context for each meeting |

---

## Tips
- **Pre-start matters**: Send welcome materials and complete IT setup before day one
- **Buddy system**: Assign an onboarding buddy separate from the manager
- **Information dosing**: Don't dump everything in week one — spread learning across 90 days
- **Feedback loops**: Schedule check-ins at day 7, 30, 60, and 90

## Troubleshooting

**Plan too overwhelming**
Say: "Limit week 1 to orientation essentials. Move technical training to weeks 2-3."

**Missing role-specific content**
Specify: "This role uses [tools/systems]. Include training on each."

**No clear success criteria**
Ask: "Define what 'fully onboarded' looks like with measurable indicators."
