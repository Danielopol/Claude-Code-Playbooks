## What This Does

Transforms your running notes and observations into complete performance reviews with specific examples, balanced feedback, rating justifications, and development plans. Saves 1-2 hours per review while producing more thoughtful, example-rich evaluations.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template

Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Gather Your Notes

Collect: running observations, goal/OKR status, project outcomes, peer feedback.

### Step 3: Start Using It

```bash
claude
```

Say: **"Write a performance review for Sarah Chen, Senior Engineer. Here are my observations and her goal status..."**

---

## Review Components

| Component | What's Generated |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Strengths** | Specific examples with measurable impact |
| **Growth Areas** | Constructive feedback with actionable next steps |
| **Goal Assessment** | Each goal rated with supporting evidence |
| **Rating Justification** | Clear rationale tied to examples |
| **Talking Points** | Conversation guide for the review meeting |
| **Development Plan** | 30/60/90 day focus areas |

---

## Tips

- **Keep running notes all year**: 10 minutes/month beats 2 hours of archaeology at review time
- **No surprises**: If growth areas haven't been discussed in 1:1s, discuss them first
- **Be specific**: "Improved API response time by 40%" beats "good technical skills"
- **Make development actionable**: Concrete next steps, not vague aspirations

## Troubleshooting

**Review sounds too positive**
Say: "Add balanced constructive feedback — at least 2 specific development areas"

**Not enough specific examples**
Provide more input: "Sarah also led the database migration which reduced costs by 30%"

**Wrong format**
Specify: "Use our company's format: Exceeds/Meets/Below for each competency"
