## What This Does

Generates structured policy and procedure documents with clear ownership, approval workflows, compliance mappings, and version tracking. Ensures consistency across organizational documentation.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template
Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Define the Policy Area
Know: what needs documenting, regulatory requirements, stakeholders, and existing practices.

### Step 3: Start Using It
```bash
claude
```
Say: **"Create a data retention policy that covers GDPR requirements for our SaaS platform"**

---

## Document Structure

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Purpose & Scope** | Why this policy exists and who it applies to |
| **Definitions** | Key terms clarified |
| **Policy Statements** | Clear rules and requirements |
| **Procedures** | Step-by-step implementation |
| **Responsibilities** | Who owns what |
| **Compliance Mapping** | Which regulations this satisfies |
| **Review Schedule** | When and how to update |

---

## Tips
- **Write for the newest employee**: Clear enough for someone on day one
- **Include examples**: Abstract policies need concrete scenarios
- **Map to regulations**: Show which requirement each section satisfies
- **Version control**: Track changes with date, author, and approval

## Troubleshooting

**Policy is too vague**
Add: "Include specific examples and scenarios for each policy statement"

**Too legalistic**
Say: "Write in plain language — a new hire should understand this"

**Missing compliance requirements**
Specify: "Map every section to specific GDPR articles"
