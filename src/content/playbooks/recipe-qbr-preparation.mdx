## What This Does

Creates structured quarterly business review packages with usage analytics, ROI documentation, achievement highlights, risk identification, and strategic recommendations — ready to present to customer stakeholders.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template
Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Gather Account Data
Compile usage metrics, support tickets, success milestones, and customer goals from the past quarter.

### Step 3: Start Using It
```bash
claude
```
Say: **"Prepare a QBR for Acme Corp. They've been a customer for 18 months, expanded to 500 seats last quarter, and their renewal is in 90 days."**

---

## QBR Structure

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Executive Summary** | Relationship health and key highlights |
| **Value Delivered** | ROI metrics and success milestones achieved |
| **Usage Analytics** | Adoption trends, feature utilization, benchmarks |
| **Support Review** | Ticket trends, resolution times, open issues |
| **Roadmap Alignment** | Upcoming features relevant to their use cases |
| **Strategic Recommendations** | Next-quarter goals and expansion opportunities |

---

## Tips
- **Lead with value delivered**: ROI metrics and outcomes before anything else
- **Benchmark against peers**: "Your adoption is 30% above average for your segment"
- **Address risks proactively**: Don't wait for the customer to raise concerns
- **End with a mutual action plan**: Both sides should leave with clear next steps

## Troubleshooting

**No clear ROI to show**
Say: "Quantify time savings and efficiency gains if revenue impact isn't measurable."

**Low usage metrics**
Ask: "Frame as adoption opportunity. Include an enablement plan with training recommendations."

**Too many slides**
Specify: "Limit to 10 slides maximum. Move supporting data to an appendix."
