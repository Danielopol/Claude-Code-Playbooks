## What This Does

Analyzes new or changing regulations to assess business impact, identify compliance gaps, estimate implementation effort, and create actionable compliance roadmaps.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template
Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Gather Regulatory Details
Have the regulation text, effective dates, and your current compliance posture.

### Step 3: Start Using It
```bash
claude
```
Say: **"Assess the impact of the new EU AI Act on our ML products. What do we need to change and by when?"**

---

## Assessment Output

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Regulation Summary** | Plain-language overview of requirements |
| **Applicability** | Which parts of your business are affected |
| **Gap Analysis** | Current state vs. required state |
| **Impact Rating** | Business impact by function (High/Med/Low) |
| **Implementation Plan** | Steps to achieve compliance |
| **Timeline** | Milestones aligned to regulatory deadlines |
| **Resource Estimate** | Budget and headcount needed |

---

## Tips
- **Start early**: Compliance projects always take longer than expected
- **Cross-functional impact**: Regulations rarely affect just one department
- **Engage legal early**: Use this for initial scoping, then validate with counsel
- **Monitor for updates**: Regulations often get amended before enforcement

## Troubleshooting

**Assessment too surface-level**
Provide: your current data practices, product architecture, and existing compliance certifications

**Regulation is ambiguous**
Flag: "Note areas of regulatory ambiguity and recommend conservative interpretation"

**Scope feels overwhelming**
Prioritize: "Phase 1: minimum viable compliance by deadline. Phase 2: best-in-class."
