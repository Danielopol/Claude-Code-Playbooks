## What This Does

Accelerates RFP responses by organizing requirements, drafting structured answers, building compliance matrices, and weaving in win themes. Reduces response time from days to hours while improving consistency and compliance.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template
Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Load the RFP Document
Place the RFP document and any past proposals in your working directory.

### Step 3: Start Using It
```bash
claude
```
Say: **"Analyze this RFP and create a compliance matrix. Then draft responses for sections 3 through 7."**

---

## Response Components

| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Compliance Matrix** | Requirement-by-requirement coverage map |
| **Win Themes** | 3-4 differentiators woven throughout |
| **Section Drafts** | Structured responses per RFP requirement |
| **Past Performance** | Relevant case studies and references |
| **Risk Mitigation** | How you'll address concerns |
| **Pricing Narrative** | Value justification for your pricing |

---

## Tips
- **Read the evaluation criteria**: Weight your effort toward highest-scored sections
- **Address every requirement**: A compliance matrix ensures nothing is missed
- **Weave win themes consistently**: Repeated differentiators build a cohesive narrative
- **Reuse and adapt**: Build a response library from successful past proposals

## Troubleshooting

**Missing requirements**
Ask: "Cross-check every RFP requirement against our compliance matrix — flag gaps"

**Responses too generic**
Provide: "Use specific metrics from our [project name] engagement"

**Compliance gaps**
Say: "We don't meet requirement 4.3 — draft an alternative approach with mitigation"
