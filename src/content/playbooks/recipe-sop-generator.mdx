## What This Does

Transforms expert knowledge and process descriptions into structured Standard Operating Procedures with numbered steps, decision trees, exception handling, and quality checklists. Ensures processes are repeatable and trainable.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template
Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Describe the Process
Walk through the process step by step, including what decisions get made and what could go wrong.

### Step 3: Start Using It
```bash
claude
```
Say: **"Create an SOP for our customer onboarding process. Here's how it currently works..."**

## SOP Components

| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Purpose** | Why this process exists |
| **Scope** | When to use (and not use) this SOP |
| **Steps** | Numbered, sequential actions |
| **Decision Points** | If/then branching logic |
| **Exceptions** | How to handle edge cases |
| **Quality Checks** | Verification at key milestones |
| **Troubleshooting** | Common problems and fixes |
| **Owner & Review** | Who maintains this, review cadence |

---

## Tips
- **Capture from the expert**: Interview the person who does it best, not the manager
- **Include the WHY**: "Verify before sending (because errors cost ~$500 each to fix)"
- **Test with a newcomer**: Have someone unfamiliar try to follow the SOP
- **Living document**: Schedule quarterly reviews to catch process drift

## Troubleshooting

**SOP is too detailed / too general**
Calibrate: "Written for someone with 3 months of experience in this role"

**Doesn't cover edge cases**
Ask: "What are the top 5 things that go wrong? Add exception handling for each"

**Process keeps changing**
Add: "Include a change log section with version dates and what changed"
