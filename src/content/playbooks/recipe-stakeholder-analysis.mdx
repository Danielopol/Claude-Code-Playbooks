## What This Does

Maps project stakeholders by influence, interest, and attitude. Designs tailored communication strategies for each group and builds engagement plans that keep the right people informed and aligned.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template
Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: List Your Stakeholders
Identify all people and groups who are affected by or can influence your project.

### Step 3: Start Using It
```bash
claude
```
Say: **"Create a stakeholder analysis for our office relocation project. Map 15 stakeholders by influence and interest, and design a communication plan."**

## Analysis Components

| Component | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| **Stakeholder Map** | Power/interest grid positioning |
| **RACI Matrix** | Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed |
| **Attitude Assessment** | Supporter, neutral, or resistor |
| **Communication Plan** | Channel, frequency, and content by stakeholder |
| **Engagement Strategy** | How to move resistors and maintain supporters |
| **Risk Identification** | Stakeholder-related project risks |

---

## Tips
- **High power + low interest**: Keep satisfied — they can block you if surprised
- **High power + high interest**: Manage closely — your key allies or biggest risks
- **Resistors need attention**: Understand their concerns before trying to change their minds
- **Update the map**: Stakeholder positions shift — revisit monthly

## Troubleshooting

**Too many stakeholders**
Say: "Group by interest and influence. Communicate at group level for low-influence stakeholders."

**Can't assess attitudes**
Ask: "Infer from their stated concerns, past project behavior, and what they stand to gain or lose."

**Communication fatigue**
Specify: "Different stakeholders need different cadences. Not everyone needs weekly updates."
