## What This Does

Designs and optimizes referral, affiliate, and ambassador programs that turn customers and partners into growth engines — covering incentive math, sharing moments, tracking, payouts, and anti-abuse.

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Program type** — customer referral, affiliate, ambassador, or combination
- **Business model** — B2B/B2C, LTV, CAC from other channels
- **Current state** — existing program, organic referrals, NPS
- **Goals** — new customers, brand reach, partner ecosystem

## How It Works

1. Choose program type(s) appropriate for your motion and audience
2. Calculate sustainable incentive structure based on LTV/CAC
3. Design the sharing moment (when, where, how customers refer)
4. Define tracking — referral codes, links, attribution rules
5. Design payout and recognition flow
6. Add anti-abuse protections
7. Plan launch and ongoing optimization

## What You Get

- Program design document with type, structure, and rationale
- Incentive math with payback math
- Sharing moment design (in-product, email, social)
- Tracking and payout flow
- Anti-abuse rules
- Launch plan and KPIs
