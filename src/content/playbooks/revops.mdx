## What This Does

Designs the systems that connect marketing, sales, and customer success — covering lead lifecycle, scoring, routing, MQL/SQL handoff, deal desk, CRM automation, and data hygiene — so leads don't leak, reports don't lie, and revenue forecasts are actually trustworthy.

Create a dedicated folder for this workflow (e.g. `~/marketing/revops`).

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above and save the file as `CLAUDE.md` in that folder.

### Step 3: Run the Workflow

Describe your GTM motion, ACV, current stack, and pain points.

---

## Inputs You'll Need

- **GTM motion** — PLG, sales-led, or hybrid
- **ACV range** and **sales cycle length**
- **Current stack** — CRM, marketing automation, scheduling, enrichment
- **Current state** — what works, where leads leak, what reports break
- **Goals** — speed-to-lead, conversion, handoff fidelity, build-from-scratch

## How It Works

1. Map the current lead lifecycle and identify leaks
2. Define lifecycle stages with shared definitions across teams
3. Build a lead scoring model grounded in real conversion data
4. Design routing rules (territory, segment, round-robin, account-based)
5. Define MQL/SQL handoff with SLA and rejection feedback loop
6. Set CRM hygiene rules and automation
7. Plan rollout, training, and review cadence

## What You Get

- Lead lifecycle map with shared definitions
- Lead scoring model with weights and rationale
- Routing rules and assignment logic
- MQL/SQL definitions with SLA template
- CRM hygiene plan and automations
- 90-day rollout plan
