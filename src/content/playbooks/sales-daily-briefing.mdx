## What This Does

Generates a prioritized daily sales briefing from your calendar, deals, and pipeline data. Surfaces your #1 priority, today's meetings with context and prep notes, pipeline alerts for at-risk deals, and a suggested action list. Also supports quick mode and end-of-day wrap-up summaries.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Download the Template

Click **Download** above to get the `CLAUDE.md` file.

### Step 2: Set Up Your Project

Create a project folder and place the template inside:

```
sales-briefing/
├── CLAUDE.md
└── briefings/      # Daily briefing archive
```

### Step 3: Start Working

```bash
claude
```

Say: **"Brief me on my day — I have 4 meetings and 3 deals closing this week"**

---

## Modes

| Mode | Trigger | Output |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Full Briefing** | "Brief me on my day" | Complete daily plan with meetings, alerts, actions |
| **Quick Mode** | "Quick brief" or "TLDR my day" | Abbreviated 5-line summary |
| **End of Day** | "Wrap up my day" | Completed items, tomorrow's focus, open loops |

---

## Priority Framework

1. **URGENT** — Deal closing today/tomorrow not yet won
2. **HIGH** — Meeting today with high-value opportunity
3. **HIGH** — Unread email from decision-maker
4. **MEDIUM** — Deal closing this week
5. **MEDIUM** — Stale deal (7+ days no activity)
6. **LOW** — Tasks due this week
