## What This Does

Turns Claude into a sales engineering partner that builds technical discovery question frameworks, generates RFP/RFI responses with win themes and differentiators, prepares demo scripts with pain-point-to-feature mapping, plans POCs with measurable success criteria, creates competitive objection handling playbooks, and drafts technical architecture diagrams for proposals.

---

## The Problem

Sales engineers juggle dozens of deals at once. Every RFP demands hours of copy-pasting from outdated response libraries. Demo prep means scrambling to remember which features map to which pain points. POC planning is ad-hoc with vague success criteria that let deals stall. Competitive intel lives in someone's head, not in a structured playbook the whole team can use.

---

## The Fix

Give Claude your product capabilities, competitor landscape, and prospect context. It builds structured discovery question trees, generates tailored RFP responses that highlight your differentiators, creates demo run-of-show documents with talk tracks tied to specific pain points, and produces POC plans with clear milestones and go/no-go criteria.

## Discovery Question Framework

Claude generates tiered discovery questions:

| Phase | Focus | Example Questions |
|-------|-------|-------------------|
| **Level 1: Situation** | Current state | "Walk me through your current workflow for X" |
| **Level 2: Problem** | Pain identification | "Where does that process break down at scale?" |
| **Level 3: Implication** | Business impact | "What does that cost you in hours/revenue per quarter?" |
| **Level 4: Technical** | Architecture fit | "How does your current stack handle Y integration?" |
| **Level 5: Vision** | Future state | "If you could redesign this from scratch, what would it look like?" |

## Demo Script Builder

Claude creates demo run-of-show documents:

- **Opening hook**: Pain point restatement in their words
- **Feature sequence**: Ordered by prospect priority, not product menu
- **Talk track**: What to say at each transition
- **Wow moments**: Pre-planned spots that map to their top 3 pain points
- **Objection traps**: Setups that preempt competitor claims
- **Close sequence**: Summary tied back to business outcomes

## Tips

- **Lead with their pain, not your features**: Discovery questions should uncover business impact before you demo anything. Claude orders features by prospect priority.
- **Win themes over feature lists**: Every RFP answer should reinforce 2-3 win themes consistently. Tell Claude your themes upfront.
- **Demo the delta**: Focus demo time on what differentiates you. Claude flags commodity features to speed through and differentiators to linger on.
- **POC success criteria must be measurable**: Avoid vague criteria like "easy to use." Claude forces quantifiable metrics like "admin can configure X in under 10 minutes."
- **Competitive intel decays fast**: Update your competitor notes quarterly. Claude timestamps claims so you know what is stale.
- **Tailor by persona**: A CTO demo differs from a VP Ops demo. Tell Claude who is in the room.

## Troubleshooting

**RFP responses sound generic**
Add more product specifics: "Here is our actual capability matrix for this area, rewrite using our terminology"

**Demo script does not flow naturally**
Provide transition context: "The prospect cares most about X, then Y, then Z - reorder the flow"

**POC criteria too easy or too hard**
Calibrate with past data: "Here is what a successful POC looked like for a similar customer, use comparable metrics"

**Competitive objections feel weak**
Feed real battle cards: "Here is what [Competitor] actually claims on their site, build counter-arguments"

**Discovery questions too surface-level**
Add industry context: "These are [industry] buyers - they already know the basics, go deeper on [technical area]"
