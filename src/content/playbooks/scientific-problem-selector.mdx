## What This Does

This playbook provides a structured, conversational framework for choosing scientific research problems. Based on Fischbach and Walsh's "Problem choice and decision trees in science and engineering" (Cell, 2024), it guides researchers through idea generation, risk assessment, optimization, and decision-tree navigation -- helping you invest more time choosing the right problem before spending years solving it.

The playbook meets scientists where they are with three starting modes:

1. **Pitch an Idea** -- Work up a new project idea together through ideation, risk assessment, and impact evaluation
2. **Troubleshoot a Problem** -- Navigate obstacles in a current project using decision trees, adversity response, and problem inversion
3. **Ask a Strategic Question** -- Get structured guidance on research direction using the full framework

---

## Core Framework Concepts

**Problem Choice >> Execution Quality** -- Even brilliant execution of a mediocre problem yields incremental impact. Good execution of an important problem yields substantial impact.

**The Risk Paradox** -- Don't avoid risk; befriend it. No risk means incremental work. Multiple miracles means you need to refine. The sweet spot is understood, quantified, manageable risk.

**The Parameter Paradox** -- Too many fixed constraints creates brittleness. Too few creates paralysis. Fix ONE meaningful constraint and let others float.
