## What This Does

Turns link building from random cold emails into a repeatable system. Analyzes your backlink profile, finds gaps versus competitors, identifies high-value link opportunities, generates personalized outreach templates, and tracks your domain authority growth over time.

---

## The Problem

Link building is the most important and most neglected part of SEO. You know backlinks matter, but you are stuck in a loop of sending generic outreach emails to random sites, getting ignored, and eventually giving up. Meanwhile, your competitors keep climbing the rankings with links you do not understand how they got. Your domain authority stagnates and organic traffic plateaus.

---

## The Fix

A structured authority-building workflow that starts with data. Audit your current backlink profile, reverse-engineer where competitors get their links, identify the best opportunities by relevance and authority, generate personalized outreach emails that actually get responses, and run systematic campaigns (broken link, guest post, HARO) that compound over time.

## Tips

- **Quality over quantity**: One DA 60+ link beats twenty DA 15 links
- **Relevance matters more than DA**: A relevant DA 35 link outperforms an irrelevant DA 60 link
- **Anchor text diversity is critical**: Over-optimized anchors trigger penalties
- **Track link velocity**: Sudden spikes look unnatural to Google
- **Personalize every outreach**: Generic templates get 1-2% reply rates; personalized ones get 10-15%
- **Disavow toxic links**: Do not let spam backlinks drag down your authority

## Troubleshooting

**No backlink data available**
Google Search Console provides free backlink data. Export from Links > External links.

**Competitors have way more links**
Focus on quality gaps, not quantity: "Show me competitor links from DA 50+ sites I could also target"

**Outreach emails get ignored**
Ask: "Rewrite this pitch with a stronger value proposition for the site owner"

**Not sure which pages to build links to**
Start with: "Which of my pages rank on page 2 and would move to page 1 with 3-5 quality links?"

**Anchor text looks over-optimized**
Ask: "Audit my anchor text distribution and flag anything that looks unnatural"
