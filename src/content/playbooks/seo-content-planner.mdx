## What This Does

Turns raw keyword lists and competitor URLs into a structured editorial calendar. Claude clusters keywords by topic, maps search intent, identifies content gaps against competitors, and generates ready-to-assign content briefs complete with outlines, target word counts, and internal linking suggestions.

---

## The Problem

Content teams waste hours in spreadsheets trying to group keywords, guess search intent, and figure out what to write next. Without a system, you end up with overlapping articles, missed opportunities, and no clear publishing roadmap.

---

## The Fix

Hand Claude your keyword export (from Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Search Console, or even a brainstorm list) and your top competitor URLs. It builds a full editorial plan: clustered topics, intent-mapped keywords, gap analysis, and actionable content briefs you can hand directly to writers.

## What Gets Built

| Deliverable | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Keyword Clusters** | Groups of related keywords mapped to single content pieces |
| **Intent Map** | Each cluster tagged as informational, commercial, navigational, or transactional |
| **Gap Analysis** | Topics competitors rank for that you do not cover |
| **Editorial Calendar** | Month-by-month publishing schedule with priorities |
| **Content Briefs** | Title, outline, target keywords, word count, linking plan per article |

## Tips

- **Start with one competitor**: Analyzing too many at once creates noise; pick your closest rival first
- **Volume is not everything**: A 200-volume keyword with commercial intent can outperform a 5,000-volume informational one
- **Cluster before you plan**: Always group keywords before deciding on articles to avoid cannibalization
- **Refresh quarterly**: Search trends shift; re-run the gap analysis every 90 days
- **Include zero-volume terms**: New or niche queries often convert best and face less competition

## Troubleshooting

**No keyword data available**
Give Claude a list of topics and competitor URLs — it can reverse-engineer keyword targets from competitor content structure.

**Too many keyword clusters**
Say: "Limit to the top 20 clusters by combined search volume" to keep the calendar manageable.

**Not sure which intent category fits**
Claude defaults to the SERP layout heuristic: if top results are blog posts it is informational, if product pages it is commercial. Ask it to explain its reasoning for any cluster.

**Clusters overlap**
Ask: "Merge any clusters that could be covered in a single article" — Claude will consolidate and flag potential cannibalization risks.
