## What This Does

Reduces friction and increases completion rates in signup, registration, and trial activation flows — covering free trial signups, freemium, paid account creation, waitlist, and B2B vs B2C nuances.

---

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above and save the file as `CLAUDE.md` in that folder.

### Step 3: Run the Workflow

Share the current signup flow (screenshots, URLs, or HTML) and any drop-off data.

---

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Flow type** — free trial, freemium, paid, waitlist, B2B/B2C
- **Current flow** — pages, fields, steps
- **Performance data** — completion rate, drop-off step, source traffic
- **Tech constraints** — auth providers, CRM/marketing automation requirements

## How It Works

1. Map the current flow and drop-off points
2. Cut every field that isn't required to activate
3. Decide single-step vs multi-step (or progressive profiling)
4. Add social auth where appropriate
5. Improve micro-copy, errors, and confirmations
6. Audit mobile experience
7. Recommend A/B tests with hypotheses

## What You Get

- Field-by-field audit
- Recommended flow with cut/keep/defer decisions
- Social auth + SSO recommendations
- Mobile-specific changes
- Error/recovery flow improvements
- A/B test backlog
