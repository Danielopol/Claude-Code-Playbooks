## What This Does

Helps create engaging social content — single posts, threads, carousels, and short-form video scripts — across LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts. Covers content calendars, hook libraries, repurposing, and platform-specific formats.

## Inputs You'll Need

- **Goals** — brand, leads, traffic, community
- **Audience** — who you want to reach
- **Voice** — personal brand vs company brand vs both
- **Existing content** — what's working, what isn't
- **Platforms** — LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube Shorts

## How It Works

1. Define point of view and 3–5 content pillars
2. Pick repeatable formats per platform (LinkedIn carousel, Twitter thread, Reel hook, etc.)
3. Build a hook library tailored to the platform's ranking signals
4. Plan 30 days of posts mixing pillars and formats
5. Create a repurposing flow (1 long-form → many social pieces)
6. Define KPIs per platform

## What You Get

- POV + content pillars
- Repeatable format playbook per platform
- 30-day calendar with hooks
- Repurposing flow
- KPIs per platform with what to actually optimize for
