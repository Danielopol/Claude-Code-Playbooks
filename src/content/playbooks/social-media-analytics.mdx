## What This Does

Turns scattered social media metrics into clear performance intelligence. Pulls together data from Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Facebook to track campaign ROI, benchmark engagement against industry standards, find your best posting windows, and generate weekly or monthly reports your team can actually act on.

---

## The Problem

Social media teams drown in platform-specific dashboards. Instagram says one thing, LinkedIn says another, and nobody agrees on what "engagement" means. You spend hours copying numbers into spreadsheets, your ROI calculations are guesswork, and leadership keeps asking "is social media actually working?" without getting a straight answer.

---

## The Fix

A unified analytics workflow that standardizes metrics across platforms, calculates true campaign ROI (including labor costs), benchmarks your performance against industry averages, and outputs clean reports. You provide the exports, Claude does the math and the storytelling.

## What Gets Analyzed

| Area | Metrics Tracked |
|------|----------------|
| **Engagement** | Likes, comments, shares, saves, clicks, replies |
| **Reach** | Impressions, reach, follower growth rate |
| **Conversion** | Link clicks, UTM tracking, landing page visits |
| **ROI** | Ad spend, organic value, cost per engagement |
| **Content** | Post type performance, format comparison |
| **Timing** | Best day/hour, frequency impact |

## Tips

- **Export consistently**: Same date ranges across platforms for accurate comparison
- **Track UTMs religiously**: Without them, conversion attribution is guesswork
- **Engagement rate > follower count**: A 5% rate on 1K followers beats 0.5% on 50K
- **Benchmark by industry**: B2B and B2C averages differ wildly
- **Include labor costs in ROI**: Content creation time is a real expense
- **Save historical data**: Trend analysis requires at least 3 months of data

## Troubleshooting

**Missing platform data**
Claude can work with partial data. Specify which platforms you have: "Analyze only Instagram and LinkedIn"

**Engagement rates seem low**
Ask: "What's the industry benchmark for [your niche] on [platform]?" — your rate may be normal

**ROI is hard to calculate for organic**
Claude can estimate organic post value using equivalent ad cost: "Calculate organic value using $X CPM benchmark"

**Too much data to process**
Start focused: "Just analyze my top 10 posts this month and tell me what they have in common"

**Competitor data is incomplete**
Public metrics only: "Benchmark against [competitor] using their public post engagement"
