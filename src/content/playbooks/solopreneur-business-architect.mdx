## What This Does

Get a complete blueprint for building a profitable one-person business using AI as your leverage. Claude analyzes your situation and provides an exact business model, offer design, pricing strategy, client acquisition system, and 90-day action plan.

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Create a Business Planning Folder

```bash
mkdir -p ~/Documents/BusinessBlueprint
```

### Step 2: Download the Template

Click **Download** above, then:
```bash
mv ~/Downloads/CLAUDE.md ~/Documents/BusinessBlueprint/
```

### Step 3: Fill In Your Situation

Open CLAUDE.md and fill in:
- Your skills
- Available capital
- Time per week
- Background/experience
- Risk tolerance

### Step 4: Get Your Blueprint

```bash
cd ~/Documents/BusinessBlueprint
claude
```

Then say: **"Build me a $100K one-person business blueprint"**

## Tips

- **Be honest about your situation**: Accurate input = accurate blueprint
- **Start with service arbitrage**: Fastest path to first dollar
- **Price for margin, not volume**: Fewer clients at higher prices
- **Document everything**: SOPs make scaling possible
