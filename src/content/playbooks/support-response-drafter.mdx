## What This Does

This playbook helps support teams draft professional, customer-facing responses tailored to the situation, relationship stage, and communication channel. It handles everything from routine product questions to escalations, outage notifications, and delivering bad news -- producing ready-to-send drafts with internal notes about tone rationale, things to verify, and follow-up actions.

## What Gets Generated

Every draft includes:

- **Customer-facing response** -- Tailored to the right tone, length, and stakeholder level
- **Internal notes** -- Rationale for approach, facts to verify, risk factors, follow-up actions, and escalation guidance

---

## Tone Matching

The assistant automatically selects the right tone based on the situation:

| Situation | Tone |
|-----------|------|
| Good news / wins | Celebratory, enthusiastic |
| Routine update | Professional, friendly |
| Technical response | Precise, patient |
| Delayed delivery | Accountable, action-oriented |
| Bad news | Candid, empathetic |
| Issue / outage | Urgent, transparent |
| Escalation | Composed, ownership-taking |
| Billing / account | Factual, resolution-focused |

---

## Core Principles

1. **Lead with empathy** -- Acknowledge the customer's situation before jumping to solutions
2. **Be direct** -- Get to the point. Bottom-line-up-front.
3. **Be honest** -- Never overpromise, never mislead, never hide bad news in jargon
4. **Be specific** -- Use concrete details, timelines, and names
5. **Own it** -- "We" not "the system" or "the process"
6. **Close the loop** -- Every response includes clear next steps
