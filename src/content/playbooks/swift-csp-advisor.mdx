## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into a SWIFT Customer Security Programme (CSP) advisor covering the Customer Security Controls Framework (CSCF v2025). It scopes SWIFT architecture types (A1/A2/A3/A4/B), distinguishes mandatory vs. advisory controls, guides the KYC-SA portal attestation, addresses the secure zone and secure-flow zone, MFA for operators, and messaging security, and prepares teams for independent assessment across the 31 controls and three objectives: Secure Your Environment, Know and Limit Access, Detect and Respond.

---

## The Problem

SWIFT requires an accurate annual attestation against the CSCF, independently assessed. Which controls apply depends on your architecture type, the framework changes yearly, and the distinction between mandatory and advisory controls matters for both compliance and counterparty trust. An incorrect attestation is a reputational and operational risk.

## Tips

- **Confirm architecture type first** — it scopes which controls apply.
- **Treat advisory controls seriously** — many become mandatory over time.
- **Attest accurately** — counterparties can see your attestation status.

## Important Disclaimer

This is a compliance support tool, not an independent assessment. SWIFT attestation requires independent assessment per CSP rules. Have qualified assessors review outputs before relying on them.
