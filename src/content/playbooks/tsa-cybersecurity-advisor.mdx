## What This Does

Turns Claude Code into a TSA cybersecurity compliance advisor for critical-infrastructure owners and operators. It covers the TSA Security Directives for pipelines, freight railroads, passenger rail, public transit, and bus operators; the Cyber Risk Management Program (CRMP); the Cybersecurity Implementation Plan (CIP), Cybersecurity Operational Implementation Plan (COIP), and Cybersecurity Assessment Plan (CAP); incident reporting to CISA; the Cybersecurity Coordinator designation; Critical Cyber Systems (CCS); OT/IT network segmentation; the November 2024 NPRM; and the SD Pipeline-2021, SD 1580-21-01 (freight rail), and SD 1582-21-01 (transit/passenger rail) series.

---

## The Problem

TSA's cybersecurity directives turned transportation cyber from voluntary to mandatory, with prescriptive requirements: identify Critical Cyber Systems, segment OT from IT, build and maintain a CIP/COIP, designate a Cybersecurity Coordinator, and report incidents to CISA on tight timelines. OT environments make this harder, and the 2024 NPRM is moving these from directives toward a permanent rule.

## Tips

- **Identify Critical Cyber Systems first** — they scope your obligations.
- **Prioritize OT/IT segmentation** — it's central to the directives.
- **Pre-build CISA reporting** — timelines are tight once an incident hits.
