## What This Does

Turns Claude into an editorial director who generates 30-50 original, execution-ready content ideas by crossing your pillars with your extracted frameworks, audience pain points, and current trends. Every idea comes as a complete Idea Card with a working title, hook draft, and confidence rating — mapped into a 30-day content calendar.

This is **Part 7 of 10** in the Content Swipe File System series by @hooeem.

---

## Why This Works

This is the payoff moment where strategy becomes output. The protocol:

- **Framework x Pillar Matrix** — Systematically crosses frameworks with pillars for guaranteed idea volume
- **Pain Point Expansion** — 4 angles per pain point (solution, contrarian, story, myth-busting)
- **Specificity Escalator** — Pushes every idea from generic to "this was written for ME"
- **Complete Idea Cards** — Not just topics but hooks, frameworks, formats, and confidence ratings
- **Calendar mapping** — Balanced across pillars, formats, and platforms

| Field | Description |
|-------|------------|
| **Title** | Compelling working title (specific, not generic) |
| **Pillar** | Which content pillar it maps to |
| **Framework** | Which extracted framework to use |
| **Platform** | Best platform for this idea |
| **Format** | Thread, carousel, video, article, etc. |

| **Audience Pain Point** | What pain this addresses |

| **Creation Time** | Estimated minutes to draft |

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Paste your actual frameworks** — Generic frameworks produce generic ideas. Use the specific ones from Part 6.
2. **List real pain points** — Don't guess. Use language your audience actually uses.
3. **Include trends** — Timely ideas tend to outperform evergreen ones in the short term
4. **Reject generic ideas ruthlessly** — If "10 Tips for Success" shows up, push for specificity
5. **The hook draft is the test** — If the first 2 lines don't stop your scroll, the idea needs work

## Troubleshooting

**Ideas feel repetitive**
Too many use the same framework. Ask Claude to vary frameworks across ideas.

**Ideas are too niche to execute**
The specificity escalator may have gone too far. Ask Claude to back up one level.

**Calendar is unbalanced**
Specify constraints: "No pillar should dominate more than 40%. Alternate formats across days."

**Not enough ideas for one platform**
Ask Claude to generate platform-specific batches: "Give me 15 ideas specifically for Twitter threads."
