## What This Does

A craft reference for writing anything an agent reads and follows every run — a skill, a `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md`, a reference doc reached by a pointer. The core insight: the packaging differs, but the writing doesn't, because the agent takes the same *process* each time rather than producing a one-off human-readable output. That means the same handful of levers — how a pointer is worded, where material sits on the information hierarchy, how sharp a completion criterion is, whether a concept is anchored with a leading word — decide whether the document actually gets followed.

It covers context pointers (a pointer's wording decides whether it fires, not its target), the information hierarchy of in-file steps versus in-file reference versus disclosed reference, when premature completion happens and how to defend against it, leading words as a token-efficient way to anchor behavior, and a concrete pruning discipline for hunting duplication, staleness, and no-ops sentence by sentence.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Sharpen a pointer's wording before you inline the material behind it — a must-have target behind a weakly worded pointer is a variance bug, and the cheap fix is almost always wording, not restructuring.
- When a `CLAUDE.md` has grown long and the model seems to miss rules buried in the middle, that's the sprawl failure mode. Restructure with the information hierarchy — don't just trim words from individual sentences.
- Reach for an existing word before coining your own leading word. A pretrained concept (*fog of war*, *tracer bullet*) anchors behavior for free; a made-up word costs you the definition tokens a real one gives away.
- Run the no-op test on every sentence during a pruning pass: does it change behavior versus what the model already does by default? If not, delete the whole sentence rather than trimming it.

## Limitations

- This is a craft reference, not a linter — applying it well still takes judgment about where each specific piece of content belongs on the hierarchy.
- Pairs well with, but doesn't replace, a dedicated tool for restructuring an already-bloated `CLAUDE.md` — this playbook is about writing and organizing from the ground up, not a mechanical two-level split.
- Best suited to documents an agent reads repeatedly across many runs; a truly one-off instruction doesn't need this level of engineering.
