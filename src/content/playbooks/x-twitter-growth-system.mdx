## What This Does

Provides a complete, milestone-based growth system for X (Twitter) that scales with your follower count. Instead of time-based goals, it focuses on what actually matters: reaching follower milestones through daily actions, engagement strategies, and cross-platform distribution.

## Prerequisites

- Claude Code installed
- An X (Twitter) account
- Content to share (products, playbooks, insights, etc.)
- Commitment to daily consistency

## Setup Instructions

### Step 1: Download the Template

Download the CLAUDE.md template below and save it to your marketing folder.

### Step 2: Identify Your Current Milestone

Determine which milestone you're at based on your current follower count:
- **Milestone 1**: 0 - 200 followers
- **Milestone 2**: 200 - 500 followers
- **Milestone 3**: 500 - 1,000 followers
- **Milestone 4**: 1,000+ followers

### Step 3: Start Your Daily Actions

Use Claude to help you:
- Generate engagement comments
- Draft posts and threads
- Create DM templates
- Plan your content calendar

## The System Summary

1. **Connect** - Follow people in your niche, follow-for-follow works early
2. **Reply guy forever** - Comment on big and small creators, every reply must add value
3. **Post 2-5x daily** - Tweets, threads, articles. More content = more surface area
4. **DM people** - Real conversations, real value, real relationships
5. **Cross-platform** - Reddit, LinkedIn, Slack, Discord where your audience is
6. **Engagement group** - Mutual support with other creators at your level
7. **Daily consistency** - The algorithm rewards showing up every day
8. **Stay unique** - No AI slop. People follow people, not templates

## Daily Foundation (All Milestones)

These actions never change:
- Leave 10 genuine comments on posts in your niche
- Send 3-5 DMs with value (never pitch)
- Reply to every comment on your posts FAST

## Best Practices

1. **Peak posting times**: 7-9 AM and 5-7 PM ET
2. **Reply within 3 hours** to comments on your posts
3. **One playbook = one post** - You have content, use it
4. **Screenshot user wins** and share them
5. **Record Loom walkthroughs** to show your product working
